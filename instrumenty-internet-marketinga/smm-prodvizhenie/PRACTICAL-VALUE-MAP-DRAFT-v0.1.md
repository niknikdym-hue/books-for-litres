# PRACTICAL VALUE MAP DRAFT v0.1 — «SMM продвижение»

**Status:** DRAFT / PRE-ARCHITECTURE  
**Date:** 2026-09-07

## Governing rule

Every major section/chapter must connect:

`PROBLEM → DECISION → ACTION → ARTIFACT / OUTPUT → OBSERVABLE CHECK → NEXT DECISION`.

If a chapter cannot produce a unique practical/intellectual result, disposition = `REWORK | MERGE | DELETE`.

## PV1. Is SMM strategically justified?

- **Problem:** activity exists without a business role.
- **Decision:** `PRIMARY | SUPPORTING | MINOR | NOT NOW` role for SMM.
- **Action:** map buying process and required social jobs.
- **Artifact:** `SMM Business Role Brief`.
- **Check:** every social activity can be traced to a named business/buyer job.
- **Next:** choose archetype and buyer structure.

## PV2. What am I actually selling and how is it bought?

- **Problem:** generic SMM advice ignores purchase mechanics.
- **Decision:** offer/sales archetype.
- **Action:** classify frequency, consideration, risk, buying unit, geography, capacity, proof burden, purchase path.
- **Artifact:** `Offer/Sales Archetype Diagnosis`.
- **Check:** reader can explain why their playbook differs from at least one neighboring archetype.
- **Next:** map buyer/journey.

## PV3. Who buys and what must happen before purchase?

- **Problem:** “target audience” is too shallow.
- **Decision:** buyer/buying-unit and journey structure.
- **Action:** identify initiator/influencer/decision maker/payer and critical information/proof needs.
- **Artifact:** `Buyer / Buying-Unit Map` + `SMM Journey Role Map`.
- **Check:** social role is defined at each materially relevant stage; no fake full-funnel coverage.
- **Next:** choose platforms.

## PV4. Which Russian platforms deserve resources?

- **Problem:** platform choice follows fashion/reach lists.
- **Decision:** `USE | TEST | DO NOT USE | RECHECK` for each candidate platform.
- **Action:** pass 8 filters: access, organic, paid, legal, audience, archetype, measurement, freshness.
- **Artifact:** `Russia Platform Portfolio`.
- **Check:** every chosen platform has a job; every rejected major platform has a reason.
- **Next:** proof/message/content jobs.

## PV5. What must the buyer believe/verify?

- **Problem:** vague “build trust”.
- **Decision:** required proof by buying risk/archetype.
- **Action:** map fears, uncertainty, evidence available before purchase and honest limits.
- **Artifact:** `Trust / Proof Map`.
- **Check:** each key barrier has either evidence, process proof, social proof, demonstration or explicit non-guarantee.
- **Next:** content jobs.

## PV6. What should content actually do?

- **Problem:** content calendar/rubrics replace strategy.
- **Decision:** content jobs and priority.
- **Action:** assign recurring content types to memory/proof/education/demonstration/objection/community/action functions.
- **Artifact:** `Content Jobs Portfolio`.
- **Check:** no recurring content type exists only because “we need to post”.
- **Next:** formats/production/distribution.

## PV7. How should content be produced without becoming commodity?

- **Problem:** high volume, low distinctiveness; AI slop.
- **Decision:** production priorities, reuse rules, human/AI roles.
- **Action:** define source/evidence/voice/distinctive-asset requirements and repurposing limits.
- **Artifact:** `Content Operating System` + `AI Use / Risk Map`.
- **Check:** content remains attributable, evidence-safe and functionally distinct after AI assistance.
- **Next:** distribution.

## PV8. How does content reach people?

- **Problem:** organic-only wishful thinking or paid-only dependency.
- **Decision:** organic/paid/creator/community roles.
- **Action:** choose distribution mechanisms by job, archetype, legal availability and economics.
- **Artifact:** `Distribution Mix`.
- **Check:** each distribution mode has a purpose and budget/effort rationale.
- **Next:** conversion/handoff.

## PV9. How does attention become action/revenue?

- **Problem:** views/followers without business outcome.
- **Decision:** CTA/conversion/handoff path.
- **Action:** connect social to checkout, message, booking, consultation, qualified lead, offline visit or sales process.
- **Artifact:** `Conversion & Handoff Design`.
- **Check:** reader can trace a realistic path from social contact to business outcome with major friction points named.
- **Next:** measurement.

## PV10. What should be measured?

- **Problem:** vanity metrics / false attribution certainty.
- **Decision:** metrics by SMM job.
- **Action:** define leading signal, business outcome, attribution limit and review threshold.
- **Artifact:** `SMM Measurement Card`.
- **Check:** no metric survives without a decision it can influence.
- **Next:** diagnose and iterate.

## PV11. What do I change next?

- **Problem:** random optimization.
- **Decision:** `STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE`.
- **Action:** diagnose failure layer: role, platform, proof, content, distribution, conversion, measurement, capacity/economics.
- **Artifact:** `Experiment / Decision Queue`.
- **Check:** every test has hypothesis, outcome signal and next-decision rule.
- **Next:** operating cadence.

## PV12. How do I keep the system current?

- **Problem:** platform/legal advice decays.
- **Decision:** what needs re-check and when.
- **Action:** record time-sensitive assumptions and sources.
- **Artifact:** `Freshness Register`.
- **Check:** no time-sensitive platform/legal fact enters Literary Master without current verification.

## Course completion artifact

The final practical product is not 12 disconnected worksheets. They must compose into one reader-owned **SMM Operating System**:

`ROLE + ARCHETYPE + BUYER/JOURNEY + PLATFORM PORTFOLIO + PROOF + CONTENT JOBS + DISTRIBUTION + CONVERSION/HANDOFF + MEASUREMENT + DECISION QUEUE + FRESHNESS`.

The book passes `COURSE-IN-A-BOOK COMPLETENESS` only if a reader can build this system for their own product/service without needing a separate paid course to understand the core process.

## No-padding rule

Practical artifacts are not permission to inflate the book. If two artifacts duplicate the same decision, merge them. If an artifact merely restates the chapter, delete it.
