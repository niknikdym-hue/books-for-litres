# AI-SUBSTITUTION GATE v0.1 — «SMM продвижение»

**Status:** ACTIVE GATE / PRE-DEFINITION  
**Date:** 2026-09-07

## 1. Why this gate exists

A weak SMM book is now trivial to replace with a strong AI answer.

A current AI can already produce in seconds:
- goals;
- target-audience descriptions;
- platform lists;
- content pillars;
- sample calendars;
- post ideas;
- generic funnel stages;
- engagement tactics;
- basic ad advice;
- standard metrics;
- generic “test and iterate” guidance.

Therefore none of these, by themselves, justify a 300–350k-character book.

## 2. Strong generic AI answer — expected baseline

A competent bounded AI answer to «Как продвигать товар или услугу через SMM?» will usually say something like:

1. define goals and audience;
2. select relevant platforms;
3. optimize profile/page;
4. build content strategy/pillars;
5. publish consistently;
6. engage community;
7. use ads/influencers;
8. drive traffic/leads;
9. track engagement/conversions;
10. test and improve.

This is useful but insufficient for this book.

## 3. What the book must provide beyond AI baseline

The book requires `REWORK` unless it materially exceeds the baseline in all core dimensions below.

### A. Diagnostic depth

AI baseline: “know your audience / choose platform”.

Book requirement:
- diagnose product vs service;
- diagnose purchase frequency / consideration / risk;
- diagnose buying unit;
- diagnose geography/capacity;
- diagnose proof burden;
- diagnose purchase/sales handoff;
- route reader to the correct offer/sales archetype.

### B. Evidence-backed world synthesis

AI baseline: often blends familiar principles without provenance or boundaries.

Book requirement:
- named world schools/authors where materially used;
- actual evidence function;
- competing views;
- limitations;
- explicit Elena Dym conclusion;
- no fake originality.

### C. Russia-current execution

AI baseline: often recommends global platforms generically or uses stale availability assumptions.

Book requirement:
- real Russia platform matrix;
- access ≠ organic ≠ paid ≠ legal ≠ audience fit;
- regulator/platform freshness;
- no workarounds around blocked/restricted platforms;
- pre-master recheck.

### D. Archetype-specific playbooks

AI baseline: swaps industry examples into one generic framework.

Book requirement:
- Snickers ≠ industrial machine;
- manicure ≠ tutor;
- shared evergreen logic explained once;
- only material deltas repeated;
- platform/content/proof/conversion/measurement vary when mechanism varies.

### E. Full practical outputs

AI baseline: recommendations.

Book requirement: the reader creates and validates an operating system:
- SMM Business Role Brief;
- Offer/Sales Archetype Diagnosis;
- Buying-Unit Map;
- SMM Journey Role Map;
- Russia Platform Portfolio;
- Trust/Proof Map;
- Content Jobs Portfolio;
- Distribution Mix;
- Conversion/Handoff Design;
- Measurement Card;
- Experiment/Decision Queue;
- Freshness Register.

### F. Decision quality under uncertainty

AI baseline: “test what works”.

Book requirement:
- define what “works” means by job;
- distinguish brand/future-demand signals from direct-response outcomes;
- state attribution limits;
- provide `STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE` decision logic;
- prevent vanity metrics from becoming business truth.

### G. Cross-chapter progression

AI baseline: list answer can be read in any order.

Book requirement:
- each chapter enables a new decision that depends on earlier decisions;
- deleting a chapter must remove a unique necessary step or it should be deleted/merged;
- mid-book audit prevents drift and duplicated mechanisms.

### H. Human author judgment

AI baseline: synthetic consensus voice.

Book requirement:
- Elena Dym takes explicit positions;
- explains why;
- names boundary conditions;
- distinguishes evidence from judgment;
- translates the conclusion to Russia and archetypes;
- remains recognizably responsible for the decision system.

## 4. Chapter-level substitution test

Before `WRITING_ALLOWED=YES`, every chapter must answer:

1. What unique reader decision/function does this chapter provide?
2. Could a strong AI answer deliver 80%+ of this value in 1–3 pages of generic advice?
3. If yes, what additional evidence, synthesis, diagnostic branching, artifact or cross-book role makes the chapter necessary?
4. If nothing material remains, disposition = `MERGE | DELETE | REWORK`.

A chapter is not saved by:
- more examples;
- more words;
- a checklist repeating prose;
- named authors without synthesis;
- current platform screenshots;
- decorative AI commentary.

## 5. Whole-book substitution test

Before Literary Master ask a fresh current AI system the central reader question and compare it against the book.

The book must materially win on:
- diagnostic specificity;
- evidence/provenance;
- product/service archetype branching;
- Russia freshness/legal reality;
- practical artifacts;
- author judgment;
- exception/boundary handling;
- end-to-end coherence;
- measurable reader capability after completion.

If the book mainly offers a much longer version of the AI answer, final status = `NO_GO / REWORK`.

## 6. No-padding connection

AI-substitution and NO PADDING are linked.

If a section adds length but not a capability unavailable in a shorter strong answer, it is probable filler. The burden is on the section to prove a distinct function.

## 7. Gate status

Current Book Definition: `AI-SUBSTITUTION = OPEN / NOT YET PASSED`.

Pass requires:
- completed benchmark comparison;
- completed archetype matrix;
- completed Russia platform/application map;
- final Author Synthesis;
- practical-output architecture;
- chapter-level deletion/substitution rules in Architecture.
