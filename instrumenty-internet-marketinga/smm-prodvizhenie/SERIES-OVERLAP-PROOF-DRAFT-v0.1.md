# SERIES OVERLAP PROOF DRAFT v0.1 — «SMM продвижение»

**Status:** DRAFT / PRE-DEFINITION  
**Date:** 2026-09-07

## 1. Purpose

Protect the first production book from consuming the intellectual territory of the two other locked titles and neighboring author series.

A short prerequisite/cross-reference is allowed. Rebuilding another book inside SMM is prohibited.

## 2. SMM book — candidate protected territory

Central job:

**How to design, launch, operate, measure enough to manage, and improve an SMM system for a specific product/service in current Russian reality.**

Core territory likely includes:
- role of social in marketing/buying journey;
- offer/sales archetype diagnosis;
- product vs service SMM differences;
- platform portfolio selection;
- trust/proof/message architecture for social;
- content jobs and production system;
- organic/paid/creator/community distribution roles;
- conversion/sales handoff from social;
- SMM-operating measurement sufficient for decisions;
- diagnosis/experimentation loop;
- AI use in SMM;
- Russia platform/access/legal/freshness layer.

## 3. Boundary with «Квиз-маркетинг. Как собирать лиды, вовлекать и продавать через тесты»

### Quiz book protected territory

Deep system of interactive tests/quizzes as a mechanism for:
- engagement and progressive information capture;
- qualification;
- segmentation/personalized routing;
- lead quality vs quantity;
- quiz question/flow design;
- friction vs intent signal;
- quiz/form/chat/bot comparison in depth;
- quiz-specific experimentation and optimization;
- CRM/sales handoff from interactive diagnostics;
- use cases across channels, not only social.

### What SMM may contain

SMM may explain:
- when a social CTA should lead to a quiz/test vs form/message/booking/checkout;
- high-level decision criteria;
- one bounded example if needed.

### What SMM must NOT contain

- full quiz-building methodology;
- detailed question logic/scoring/branching;
- comprehensive quiz vs form/bot conversion system;
- a standalone lead-qualification framework whose main intellectual value belongs to the quiz book.

### Cross-reference rule

If the reader needs quiz mechanics beyond the bounded conversion decision, SMM explicitly marks it as neighboring-book territory rather than expanding.

## 4. Boundary with «Как оценивать эффективность рекламы»

### Advertising-effectiveness book protected territory

Deep decision system for marketing/ad investment under imperfect data:
- unit economics;
- attribution models and limitations;
- causality / incrementality;
- experiment design in depth;
- cross-channel comparison;
- CRM/revenue linkage;
- offline/long-cycle conversions;
- uncertainty and missing data;
- budget reallocation across media;
- formal stop/continue/scale/investigate thresholds;
- AI/automated-platform reporting and false confidence;
- comprehensive measurement stack.

### What SMM may contain

SMM must teach enough measurement to operate SMM professionally:
- metric by SMM job;
- leading vs business outcome;
- platform metric vs business result;
- basic attribution limitations;
- source/CRM handoff where relevant;
- experiment/decision loop within SMM;
- `STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE` at the level of SMM diagnosis.

### What SMM must NOT contain

- full attribution methodology;
- comprehensive incrementality system;
- deep cross-channel budget allocation;
- book-length marketing-finance measurement framework;
- advanced experimentation/statistical treatment not needed to operate SMM.

### Boundary test

If a measurement section remains useful unchanged when all social platforms are replaced by search/display/offline media, it may belong primarily to the advertising-effectiveness book and must justify inclusion.

## 5. Boundary with series candidate — paid acquisition under platform automation

Candidate territory exists in series research but is NOT yet Owner-approved as a book.

SMM may teach paid social as one distribution mode. It must not automatically absorb a full cross-channel paid-acquisition operating system covering search/display/e-com/media automation.

If research discovers a large independent paid-acquisition mechanism, park it for portfolio decision rather than expanding SMM.

## 6. Boundary with series candidate — content differentiation/distribution in AI era

SMM must teach enough content differentiation for social effectiveness and AI-slop avoidance.

It must not automatically consume a broader content-business/editorial/distribution system spanning SEO/search/newsletters/site/multi-channel publishing unless that material is necessary for the social operating mechanism.

## 7. Boundary with series candidate — search visibility in AI era

Search/GEO/AEO/entity/source visibility is not SMM core.

Cross-channel discovery may be acknowledged only when it affects social handoff or content reuse. Deep search visibility remains separate candidate territory.

## 8. Boundary with «Секреты продвижения услуг»

That separate series owns service-specific sales/promotion systems as its own book-series territory.

SMM must explain **how service archetypes change social-media strategy** but must not become a general book on selling/promoting services outside social channels.

Allowed:
- service trust/proof in social;
- social-to-consultation/booking handoff;
- local/expert-service content jobs.

Not allowed:
- general service sales methodology unrelated to social;
- full service business positioning/sales system if social is incidental.

## 9. Boundary with marketplace series

Marketplace-specific operating systems remain separate author territory.

SMM may cover social → marketplace/e-commerce handoff where materially relevant, but not marketplace ads/listing/SEO/operations as a standalone system.

## 10. Chapter admission overlap test

Every chapter must state:
- `OWNED SMM FUNCTION`;
- `NEIGHBORING TERRITORY TOUCHED`;
- `MAXIMUM ALLOWED DEPTH`;
- `PARK / CROSS-REFERENCE` rule.

If >25–30% of a proposed chapter's unique value belongs to a protected neighboring territory, trigger `REWORK | SPLIT | PARK | DELETE` rather than simply shortening prose.

The numeric percentage is a working review heuristic, not an automatic authority decision.

## 11. Whole-book overlap pass

Before Architecture approval and before Literary Master compare:
- mechanisms;
- decision rules;
- practical artifacts;
- research functions;
- cases/examples by function;
- final reader capabilities.

The SMM book passes only if its distinctive capability remains:

**build and operate the social-media layer for a concrete product/service**

rather than:
- build quizzes;
- solve marketing attribution generally;
- run all paid acquisition;
- run marketplace operations;
- sell services generally.
