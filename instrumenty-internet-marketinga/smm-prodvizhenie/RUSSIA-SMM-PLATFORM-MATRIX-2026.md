## 1. Classification

Every platform is evaluated independently on:

- `ACCESS` — can the Russian reader/business actually use it without special circumvention as a normal operating channel;
- `ORGANIC_USE` — can business publish/build audience/community/content normally;
- `PAID_PROMOTION` — is there a normal current paid route;
- `LEGAL_COMPLIANCE` — current Russian advertising/compliance risk;
- `AUDIENCE_EVIDENCE` — who actually uses it;
- `ARCHETYPE_FIT` — which goods/services/sales models it may fit;
- `MEASUREMENT` — what business-relevant measurement is available;
- `FRESHNESS` — date/source and need for re-check.

`ACCESS ≠ PAID_PROMOTION ≠ LEGAL_PERMISSION ≠ BUSINESS_FIT`.

## 3. Current primary evidence anchors

### Mediascope

- `Conference 2025` / Nov 2025: by Oct 2025 YouTube had lost 67% of time users spent there vs Jul 2024; time increased on VK (+34%), Telegram (+8%), TikTok (+35%), RUTUBE (4x). Function: establish redistribution of attention, not business suitability.
- `От ТВ до е-ком: как устроена медиаактивность аудитории 40+ лет`, data Jan–Jul 2026: 40–54 users over-index in OK/VK; 55–69 and 70+ in Dzen/OK. Function: prove age/audience differences materially affect platform selection.
- Mediascope 2026 PMEF materials: VK is the most popular social platform among adults; TikTok among teenagers. Function: demonstrate age segmentation, not recommendation.

### VK / OK / MAX

- VK official `Для бизнеса`: VK Реклама is described as a unified promotion platform across VK projects/ad network.
- VK press release 10 Jun 2026: VK Реклама added channel promotion for VK, MAX and Dzen.
- Official OK business/help pages updated in 2025–2026 confirm business groups, ad cabinet and native-ad mechanisms.
- Official MAX business-channel bot provides channels for legal entities, individual entrepreneurs and self-employed users.

### Dzen

- Yandex Advertising current page for advertising on dzen.ru confirms active display inventory and monthly audience claims; exact figures/formats/prices are `TIME_SENSITIVE` and must not enter manuscript without final re-check.

### RUTUBE

- official RUTUBE business advertising page (2026) confirms paid video advertising;
- official promotion offer effective 17 Mar 2026 confirms paid algorithmic promotion products;
- exact audience, budgets, formats and tariff numbers are `TIME_SENSITIVE`.

### Telegram / YouTube / restricted-resource advertising

- FAS clarification dated 25 Mar 2026: FAS analyses Telegram/YouTube advertising case-by-case and stated a transition period through end-2026 in which liability measures for advertising on those resources would not be applied; businesses need time to reorient.
- The same clarification says enforcement continues for resources restricted earlier, including Instagram/Facebook and VPN services; advertising on those resources is not permitted.

This area is legally sensitive. Final book language must be verified against current primary law/regulator material near publication; no legal advice or blanket “allowed” statement.

## 4. Archetype fit — research hypotheses, NOT conclusions

### Mass/FMCG goods
Potentially stronger roles:
- broad-reach video/social environments;
- distinctive assets / salience;
- creator/entertainment/distribution mechanics;
- retail/e-commerce handoff.

Must test: VK, RUTUBE, OK for relevant demos, Dzen in specific content jobs, MAX as emerging; no automatic lead-gen framing.

### Considered consumer goods
Potentially stronger roles:
- demonstration/comparison/proof;
- retargeting and repeated exposure where available;
- reviews/community/creator evidence;
- lead/e-commerce/offline handoff.

### Complex/high-ticket B2B goods
Potentially stronger roles:
- expertise/application content;
- narrow communities/channels;
- decision-maker education;
- case/proof;
- lead qualification and sales handoff.

Mass reach may be secondary; platform choice must follow buying committee and reachable audience.

### Local recurring services
Potentially stronger roles:
- geo-local audience;
- visual/social proof;
- appointment path;
- reviews/recommendations;
- repeat/advocacy;
- capacity-aware promotion.

### Expert/professional services
Potentially stronger roles:
- expertise/diagnostic content;
- trust and proof of method;
- consultation/trial path;
- longer consideration;
- community/reputation.

## 5. Fail-closed rules

- No platform enters a chapter as “recommended” merely because it has high reach.
- No legal claim is inferred from platform availability.
- No paid-promotion recommendation is made on a transitional/restricted platform without a current legal/compliance check.
- No platform audience percentage is reused without source period/population definition.
- No exact feature, tariff, ad format or algorithm claim survives to Literary Master without freshness re-check.
- If a platform becomes materially inaccessible/restricted between Architecture and master, revise the Russia execution layer; do not preserve stale advice for sunk-cost reasons.
