# STYLE PROFILE BRIEF v0.1 — «SMM продвижение»

**Status:** DRAFT / PREVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE LONG-FORM WRITING  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Author identity:** Елена Дым

## 1. Purpose

Protect the book from two opposite failures:

1. dry academic/textbook prose that hides the practical decision;
2. generic AI/business prose that sounds smooth but contains little thought.

This file does not invent an author biography or expertise claim. It defines only editorial/prose requirements already established by the book/series authority.

## 2. Required reader experience

The reader should feel:

- “мне объясняет умный практик, который разобрался в сильнейших мировых подходах”;
- “автор показывает, где подходы расходятся и какой путь считает лучшим”;
- “я понимаю не только что делать, но и почему”;
- “я могу применить это к своему товару или услуге”;
- “мне не читают лекцию ради демонстрации эрудиции”.

## 3. Register

Working target:

- strong modern Russian business nonfiction;
- intellectually serious but readable;
- concrete before abstract when possible;
- analytical without bureaucratic jargon;
- confident only where evidence/judgment justify confidence;
- author visibly responsible for synthesis;
- no fake conversationality.

## 4. World authors in prose

Named authors/researchers should enter naturally when their identity helps the argument.

Preferred movement:

`problem → named view/evidence → limitation/disagreement → Elena Dym synthesis → Russia/archetype consequence → reader action`.

Avoid:
- parade of surnames;
- long literature-review paragraphs;
- “according to X” every few sentences;
- name-dropping in place of causal explanation.

## 5. Practical prose

Practice should not be boxed away as “workbook exercises” after theory.

Preferred integration:
- explain mechanism;
- show the decision point;
- contrast archetypes;
- give procedure/decision rule;
- show output/check;
- continue argument.

Lists are allowed when list structure is itself the best information architecture. Artificial listification is prohibited.

## 6. Examples

Examples must have a function:
- expose a mechanism;
- contrast product/service archetypes;
- show an edge case;
- prove a boundary;
- demonstrate a decision;
- reveal a measurement mistake.

Repeated examples with changed professions/products are not acceptable unless the delta itself is the lesson.

Real cases require evidence. Model/composite cases must be labeled honestly in research/production metadata and reader text where necessary.

## 7. NO PADDING prose rules

Delete/rework:
- repeated explanation after the idea is already clear;
- generic transitions that add no logic;
- motivational filler;
- chapter-opening throat clearing;
- summary that merely repeats the previous pages;
- “важно понимать”, “следует отметить”, “в современном мире” and similar empty scaffolding unless the sentence carries real content;
- unnecessary preview of what will be discussed;
- artificial rhetorical questions;
- padded conclusions.

A shorter dense chapter is better than a target-length chapter with repeated function.

## 8. Anti-AI signature

REWORK if prose shows repeated patterns such as:
- symmetric “не X, а Y” chains used mechanically;
- three-part slogan rhythms everywhere;
- generic certainty without source/argument;
- abstract nouns stacked instead of concrete causal logic;
- identical paragraph cadence;
- headings that merely rephrase the paragraph;
- excessive mini-lists;
- generic “ключ к успеху / мощный инструмент / эффективная стратегия” language;
- constant meta-commentary about what the reader “now understands”.

Anti-AI diagnostics are evidence for revision, not permission for automatic stylistic rewriting without review.

## 9. Terminology

Use professional marketing terminology when it increases precision.

Rules:
- explain a term on first important use if a strong non-specialist reader may not know it;
- keep accepted international term when Russian translation would be less precise, but do not flood prose with English for prestige;
- Russian practical language should remain natural;
- do not invent new terms when an established concept already exists unless the new distinction creates genuine decision value.

## 10. Authorial presence

Elena Dym's voice should be clearest at:
- synthesis after competing views;
- decision rules;
- boundaries / “when not to do this”;
- Russia translation;
- diagnostic distinctions;
- practical recommendation under uncertainty.

Authorial presence is not first-person frequency. A text may be strongly authored without “я” in every section.

## 11. Style preview gate

Before long-form Writing, generate 2–3 comparable previews from the **same bounded content brief** after Book Definition is approved.

Compare at least:
- intellectual clarity;
- author presence;
- rhythm/readability;
- world-source integration;
- practical density;
- anti-AI smell;
- terminology burden.

Owner selects/edits the operative Style Profile. Preview text is disposable and does not enter manuscript automatically.
