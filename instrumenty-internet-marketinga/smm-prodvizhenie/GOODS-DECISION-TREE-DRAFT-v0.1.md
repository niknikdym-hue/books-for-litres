# GOODS DECISION TREE DRAFT v0.1 — «SMM продвижение»

**Status:** DRAFT / RESEARCH-BOUND / NOT OWNER-APPROVED  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Purpose:** дать читателю профессиональный путь от типа товара и механики покупки к SMM-решениям. Не является готовой главой.

## 1. Главный принцип

Товар не получает SMM-стратегию по отраслевой наклейке. Решение строится по механике покупки:

`ECONOMICS → PURCHASE FREQUENCY → CONSIDERATION / RISK → BUYER / BUYING UNIT → DISTRIBUTION / AVAILABILITY → PROOF BURDEN → SALES PATH → ROLE OF SMM`.

Если два товара materially различаются по этим параметрам, один playbook для них запрещён.

## 2. Step 0 — нужен ли SMM как значимый канал вообще?

До выбора площадки читатель отвечает:

1. Где реально происходит продажа: retail / e-commerce / marketplace / direct checkout / consultation / sales process / tender?
2. Может ли social materially повлиять на выбор до покупки, во время выбора или после покупки?
3. Есть ли измеримый бизнес-результат, который SMM способен улучшать?
4. Есть ли экономический смысл производить и распространять social content относительно маржи, LTV, частоты покупки и стоимости дистрибуции?
5. Если SMM играет лишь вспомогательную роль — это фиксируется честно; книга не заставляет бизнес «вести соцсети» ради присутствия.

Output: **SMM Business Role Brief for Goods**.

## 3. Step 1 — частота и вовлечённость покупки

### A. Частая / низкововлечённая покупка

Признаки:
- покупка относительно рутинна;
- ошибка недорога;
- цикл выбора короткий;
- физическая/торговая доступность часто важнее долгого объяснения;
- многие покупатели не хотят «изучать бренд» перед покупкой.

Working route: **G1 MASS / FMCG / LOW-INVOLVEMENT**.

Primary SMM jobs to test:
- mental availability / salience;
- distinctive brand encoding;
- broad relevant reach and frequency;
- occasions / category entry points;
- creator/cultural participation when brand-fit exists;
- retail/e-commerce availability cues.

Do not default to:
- lead generation per post;
- длинным экспертным прогревам;
- follower growth as business outcome.

### B. Редкая / обдумываемая потребительская покупка

Признаки:
- цена/риск выше;
- есть сравнение и поиск;
- важны демонстрация, отзывы, характеристики, objections;
- buyer может возвращаться к решению несколько раз.

Working route: **G2 CONSIDERED / DURABLE CONSUMER GOOD**.

Primary SMM jobs to test:
- pre-demand awareness;
- demonstration and use cases;
- comparison criteria;
- social proof;
- objection/risk reduction;
- qualified handoff to product page/store/configurator/consultation;
- lawful re-engagement where available.

### C. Редкая / дорогая / сложная B2B-покупка

Признаки:
- длинный sales cycle;
- multiple stakeholders;
- высока цена ошибки;
- техническая/операционная применимость важнее «интересного контента»;
- покупка может идти через sales/engineering/procurement/tender.

Working route: **G3 COMPLEX / HIGH-TICKET B2B PRODUCT**.

Primary SMM jobs to test:
- category mental availability among relevant buyers;
- expertise/application knowledge;
- credible cases and technical proof;
- buyer-role-specific education;
- support of active and future demand;
- qualified sales handoff.

Do not import FMCG viral-content logic.

## 4. Step 2 — кто принимает решение?

### One-person consumer
Prioritize:
- relevance;
- salience;
- proof appropriate to risk;
- simple conversion path.

### Household / pair / parent-child
Require:
- separate influencer/decision/user roles;
- content/proof for materially different concerns;
- CTA that accounts for who actually pays/decides.

### Business owner / manager
Require:
- economic/business proof;
- implementation implications;
- decision risk;
- direct route to qualified discussion where needed.

### Buying committee
Require explicit map:
`initiator → user → technical evaluator → economic buyer → procurement/legal → final approver`.

SMM content jobs may differ by role; one generic persona is insufficient.

Output: **Buyer / Buying-Unit Map**.

## 5. Step 3 — какой главный барьер покупки?

Reader identifies the dominant barrier, not every possible objection.

### Salience / memory barrier
Typical response:
- reach;
- recognizable assets;
- repeated category-linked exposure;
- platform portfolio with sufficient audience scale.

### Understanding / relevance barrier
Typical response:
- demonstration;
- use cases;
- category education;
- comparison criteria.

### Trust / proof barrier
Typical response:
- reviews/cases;
- process/quality proof;
- expert evidence;
- third-party/creator proof where credible.

### Price/value barrier
Typical response:
- value logic;
- TCO/economics where relevant;
- comparison with alternatives;
- risk-adjusted justification.

### Implementation / operational-risk barrier
Typical response:
- application cases;
- onboarding/serviceability;
- technical proof;
- implementation path;
- expert/sales handoff.

Output: **Trust / Proof Map**.

## 6. Step 4 — где завершается путь из SMM?

Select one or more real conversion destinations:

- retail/offline store;
- e-commerce product page;
- marketplace/retailer;
- direct checkout;
- message/callback;
- configurator/calculator;
- consultation/demo;
- qualified lead;
- sales-engineering process;
- tender/procurement.

If the book cannot state the handoff, “engagement” is not accepted as an end state.

Output: **Conversion & Handoff Design**.

## 7. Step 5 — выбрать Russia-current platform portfolio

Platform selection must happen **after** archetype and SMM job.

For each candidate platform:

`ACCESS → AUDIENCE FIT → CONTENT/JOB FIT → ORGANIC CAPABILITY → PAID CAPABILITY → LEGAL/COMPLIANCE → HANDOFF CAPABILITY → MEASUREMENT → COST/PRODUCTION BURDEN → FRESHNESS`.

A popular platform may be rejected if it does not fit the buying process, legal state, economics or production capability.

Output: **Russia Platform Portfolio + explicit reject reasons**.

## 8. Step 6 — content jobs, not rubrics

Every content unit must serve one or more justified jobs:

- encode brand/category memory;
- demonstrate product/result;
- reduce uncertainty;
- prove quality/application;
- answer decision-critical question;
- create/reinforce category occasion;
- support buyer-role understanding;
- trigger qualified next action;
- support retention/advocacy where relevant.

“Новости компании”, “полезный пост”, “развлекательный пост” are not sufficient strategic categories.

Output: **Content Jobs Portfolio**.

## 9. Step 7 — distribution mix

Choose deliberately:

- organic;
- paid;
- creator/influencer;
- community;
- employee/expert distribution where relevant;
- owned-channel amplification;
- sales-team use of content for B2B.

No rule such as “organic first, ads later” or “paid is mandatory” is universal.

Output: **Distribution Mix**.

## 10. Step 8 — measurement by archetype

### G1
Emphasis:
- relevant reach/frequency;
- brand/category signals;
- availability/retail/e-com business evidence;
- campaign lift where feasible.

### G2
Emphasis:
- qualified product interactions;
- assisted/direct conversions;
- high-intent actions;
- revenue/margin where linked;
- objection/decision signals.

### G3
Emphasis:
- target-account/buying-group reach where observable;
- qualified conversations;
- influenced opportunities;
- sales-cycle movement;
- pipeline/revenue evidence with attribution limits explicit.

Output: **SMM Measurement Card**.

## 11. Step 9 — STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE

### STOP
When:
- SMM role is weak/non-economic;
- platform does not reach relevant buyers;
- legal/operational constraints make execution unsuitable;
- repeated tests show no meaningful business contribution.

### FIX
When:
- correct audience but weak proof/content/offer/handoff;
- measurement broken;
- production inconsistent with required quality.

### TEST
When:
- causal uncertainty remains;
- competing content/distribution hypotheses are plausible;
- new platform/format may fit but evidence is insufficient.

### SCALE
Only when:
- business outcome is material enough;
- measurement uncertainty is understood;
- marginal economics remain acceptable;
- content/operational capacity can support expansion.

## 12. Reader completion test for GOODS

The goods path is complete only if the reader can answer:

1. What kind of buying process does my product have?
2. What exact job should SMM perform?
3. Which Russian-current platforms fit and which do not?
4. What must content prove/do?
5. Where should social attention go next?
6. What will I measure?
7. What would make me stop, fix, test or scale?

If these answers remain generic, `PRACTICAL_VALUE = REWORK`.
