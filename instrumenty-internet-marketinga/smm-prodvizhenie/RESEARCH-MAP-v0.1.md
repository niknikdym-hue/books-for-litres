## 1. Research objective

Build the strongest defensible answer to the reader question:

**«Как профессионально продвигать товар или услугу через SMM в российской действительности?»**

Research is not a hunt for enough material to fill 300–350k characters. Every item must serve a decision, mechanism, boundary, archetype distinction, practical output or factual verification need.

## 2. Core research streams

### A. Role of SMM in marketing

Questions:
- what jobs can social media perform in a marketing system;
- what it cannot reliably do;
- how brand building, demand capture, community, service, creator influence, paid distribution and sales-support differ;
- when SMM is primary, supporting or economically unjustified.

World benchmark anchors:
- Keller / Swaminathan — strategic brand management;
- Sharp / Ehrenberg-Bass — growth, mental/physical availability;
- Binet / Field — short vs long effects;
- Quesenberry / Tuten — social-media planning frameworks.

Required Author Synthesis:
- one operating model that prevents the book from reducing SMM to posting or lead-gen.

### B. Offer / Sales Archetype Matrix

Research must separately test at minimum:

1. FMCG / mass low-involvement goods;
2. considered/durable consumer goods;
3. high-ticket / complex B2B goods;
4. local recurring services;
5. expert/professional services;
6. additional types only if they change the mechanism enough to deserve separate handling.

For every archetype investigate:
- buyer/buying unit;
- frequency and consideration length;
- risk and trust burden;
- SMM role;
- content jobs;
- proof required;
- CTA/conversion path;
- organic/paid/creator/community mix;
- retention/repeat/advocacy role;
- measurement logic;
- economics/capacity constraints;
- Russian platform fit.

Output: **Offer/Sales Archetype Matrix**.

### C. Product vs service differences

Goods research:
- low vs high involvement;
- trialability/demonstrability;
- purchase frequency;
- distribution/availability dependence;
- visual/functional proof;
- category entry points;
- retail/e-commerce/offline handoff;
- B2C vs B2B buying structures.

Services research:
- intangibility and uncertainty;
- person/brand dependence;
- trust/reputation;
- capacity and geography;
- appointment/consultation funnels;
- repeat frequency;
- proof of process/outcome;
- professional ethics/regulatory boundaries where relevant.

Output: **Goods Decision Tree** + **Services Decision Tree**.

### D. Audience / buying process / customer journey

Questions:
- who initiates, influences, approves and pays;
- what information is needed at each stage;
- when social proof matters;
- what content must reduce uncertainty vs create salience vs trigger action;
- what happens outside social platforms before/after conversion.

Output: journey pattern library by archetype.

### E. Content system

Research:
- content jobs vs content formats;
- brand memory / category entry points / distinctive assets;
- proof / demonstration / education / entertainment / diagnostic / community functions;
- creator and UGC roles;
- cadence/frequency evidence vs folklore;
- content portfolio design;
- production economics;
- AI-assisted content without commodity slop.

Output: content-job matrix, not generic rubrics/calendar.

### F. Distribution

Research:
- organic distribution;
- paid social;
- creators/influencers;
- community mechanics;
- retargeting/repeated exposure where lawful and technically available;
- cross-channel handoff.

Output: distribution decision tree by archetype and platform.

### G. Measurement and experimentation

Questions:
- business outcome vs platform metric;
- reach/frequency/attention vs engagement;
- qualified leads/sales/repeat outcomes;
- attribution limits;
- incrementality/experiments where feasible;
- channel contribution when perfect attribution is unavailable;
- `STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE` thresholds.

Boundary: do not consume the full territory of locked future book «Как оценивать эффективность рекламы». SMM book teaches enough measurement to operate SMM; deep advertising-effectiveness system remains protected.

## 5. Initial verified Russia evidence anchors

### Mediascope
- 2025 conference: by Oct 2025 YouTube time had fallen sharply from July 2024 while VK, Telegram, TikTok and RUTUBE gained time; this establishes major redistribution, not automatic business suitability.
- 2026 data for 40+ audiences: 40–54 over-index in OK and VK; 55–69 and 70+ in Dzen and OK. Platform choice therefore must depend on audience, not generic popularity.

### Odnoklassniki
- official OK business/help pages in 2026 confirm business pages, organic business communities, advertising cabinet and native advertising mechanisms.

### RUTUBE
- official 2026 business advertising pages confirm active paid advertising and content-promotion products; details such as minimum budgets/formats are time-sensitive and must be rechecked before use.

### Telegram / YouTube
- FAS clarification published March 2026 states a transition period through the end of 2026 during which liability measures for advertising on Telegram and YouTube would not be applied, while the underlying restricted-resource rule and case-by-case review remain relevant. This is a **high-risk time-sensitive legal area**, not a blanket evergreen permission.

### Instagram / Facebook
- FAS clarification continues enforcement for advertising on resources restricted earlier, including Instagram/Facebook (Meta, whose activity is prohibited in Russia). These cannot be a practical advertising foundation for this book.
