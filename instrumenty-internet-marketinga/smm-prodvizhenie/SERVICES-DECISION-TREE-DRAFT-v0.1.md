# SERVICES DECISION TREE DRAFT v0.1 — «SMM продвижение»

**Status:** DRAFT / RESEARCH-BOUND / NOT OWNER-APPROVED  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Purpose:** дать читателю профессиональный путь от типа услуги и механики доверия/покупки к SMM-решениям. Не является готовой главой.

## 1. Главный принцип

Услугу нельзя продвигать как «товар без коробки». Покупатель услуги приобретает не только обещанный результат, но и **исполнение, компетентность, риск взаимодействия, доверие к исполнителю, процесс и ограниченную производственную мощность**.

Рабочая цепочка:

`ECONOMICS / CAPACITY → BUYER / USER → RISK & INTANGIBILITY → TRUST / PROOF BURDEN → GEOGRAPHY → CONSIDERATION → SERVICE DELIVERY MODEL → CONVERSION / BOOKING / SALES HANDOFF → ROLE OF SMM`.

Если две услуги materially различаются по этой цепочке, один SMM-playbook для них запрещён.

## 2. Step 0 — какую бизнес-функцию вообще должен выполнять SMM?

До выбора площадок читатель отвечает:

1. Нужно ли создавать новый спрос, перехватывать активный спрос или поддерживать доверие перед покупкой?
2. Какова реальная производственная мощность: сколько клиентов можно обслужить без падения качества?
3. Является ли география жёстким ограничением или услуга продаётся дистанционно?
4. Как покупатель снижает риск до покупки?
5. Как выглядит реальная конверсия: запись, консультация, пробный урок, диагностика, заявка, коммерческое предложение, договор?
6. Сколько стоит ошибка привлечения неподходящего клиента?
7. Какую роль играет повторная покупка / удержание / рекомендация?

Output: **SMM Business Role Brief for Services**.

## 3. Step 1 — определить базовый service archetype

### A. Local recurring service

Признаки:
- география materially ограничивает рынок;
- решение относительно быстрое;
- результат часто можно показать визуально;
- доверие важно, но покупателю не всегда нужен длинный образовательный цикл;
- есть запись/слоты/capacity;
- повторные визиты существенно влияют на экономику.

Примеры класса: мастер маникюра, салон, часть beauty/wellness/local routine services.

Working route: **S1 LOCAL RECURRING SERVICE**.

Primary SMM jobs to test:
- local discovery and reach;
- visible proof of result/consistency;
- provider trust;
- booking path;
- repeat/return triggers;
- referrals/advocacy;
- capacity balancing.

### B. Expert / professional service

Признаки:
- качество трудно оценить до покупки;
- expertise/method materially влияет на решение;
- результат может быть отложенным;
- decision cycle длиннее;
- иногда buyer и user разные;
- консультация/trial/diagnosis помогает снизить риск.

Примеры класса: репетитор, консультант, специалист, экспертная индивидуальная услуга.

Working route: **S2 EXPERT / PROFESSIONAL SERVICE**.

Primary SMM jobs to test:
- make expertise legible;
- demonstrate diagnostic thinking;
- explain method and fit/non-fit;
- build trust/proof/reputation;
- support longer consideration;
- lead to qualified consultation/trial/application.

### C. Complex B2B service

Признаки:
- multiple stakeholders / buying committee;
- длинный цикл;
- высокая цена ошибки;
- service is intangible, but implementation/process/team risk is high;
- proposal/sales process central;
- cases, expertise, delivery model and business outcomes all matter.

Examples: agency/consulting/IT implementation/professional B2B service.

Working route: **S3 COMPLEX B2B SERVICE** if research confirms material separation from S2/G3.

Primary SMM jobs to test:
- category/brand mental availability among relevant buyers;
- executive/expert credibility;
- problem framing / diagnostic authority;
- case and implementation proof;
- multi-stakeholder education;
- sales enablement and qualified handoff;
- long-cycle demand support.

### D. Experience / hospitality / local destination service — candidate

Potential examples: restaurant, studio, event, leisure/hospitality.

Admit only if research proves materially distinct mechanics:
- visual/experiential proof;
- local discovery + occasion;
- capacity/reservation;
- UGC/reviews;
- time/occasion sensitivity.

### E. Education product vs education service — boundary case

A recorded course/product may behave as a product; tutoring/coaching/cohort delivery may behave as a service. Classification follows buying/delivery mechanics, not label “education”.

## 4. Step 2 — кто покупатель, кто пользователь?

### Buyer = user
Examples: adult choosing manicure or own consultant.

Need:
- direct relevance;
- trust/proof appropriate to risk;
- clear next action.

### Buyer ≠ user
Examples: parent pays for tutor; company buyer purchases service used by team.

Need separate map:
- user problem;
- buyer concern;
- influencer/evaluator concerns;
- who sees content;
- who must be convinced;
- where conversion actually happens.

Output: **Buyer / User / Buying-Unit Map**.

## 5. Step 3 — что покупатель не может проверить до покупки?

Service marketing begins with uncertainty.

Reader identifies the dominant uncertainty:

### Result uncertainty
Need:
- credible outcomes/cases;
- realistic result boundaries;
- selection of suitable clients;
- no fabricated guarantees.

### Competence uncertainty
Need:
- diagnostic/expert content;
- method/process explanation;
- credentials/evidence where relevant;
- examples showing judgment, not merely “tips”.

### Process / experience uncertainty
Need:
- what will happen;
- service standard;
- safety/cleanliness/communication where relevant;
- onboarding/delivery visibility.

### Fit uncertainty
Need:
- who service is for/not for;
- diagnostic criteria;
- expectation setting;
- consultation/trial if useful.

### Commercial / implementation risk
Need:
- scope clarity;
- team/process;
- case proof;
- commercial logic;
- governance/implementation evidence for B2B.

Output: **Trust / Proof Architecture**.

## 6. Step 4 — geography and capacity

### Hard local geography
SMM must prioritize:
- reachable local audience;
- local proof/reviews;
- convenient booking;
- capacity utilization;
- repeat economics.

A million irrelevant views outside service area are not success.

### National / remote service
SMM may use broader expertise/discovery, but competition and proof burden rise.

### Capacity-constrained solo expert
Growth goal may be:
- higher-quality clients;
- better price/mix;
- waiting list;
- retention;
not simply “more leads”.

### Scalable team/service business
May optimize:
- qualified lead volume;
- utilization across team/locations;
- service-line demand;
- repeat/expansion.

Output: **Capacity & Geography Constraint Map**.

## 7. Step 5 — length and complexity of decision

### Short / low-risk
Examples may include some local routine services.

Need:
- proof;
- convenience;
- availability;
- low-friction booking.

### Medium / trust-heavy
Examples: tutor, specialist, higher-value expert service.

Need:
- expertise legibility;
- method;
- objections;
- fit;
- consultation/trial path.

### Long / multi-stakeholder
Complex B2B service.

Need:
- category/brand presence before active demand;
- stakeholder-specific proof;
- cases;
- thought leadership only when it demonstrates real judgment;
- sales enablement;
- qualified handoff.

## 8. Step 6 — platform portfolio for Russia

After service archetype is diagnosed, evaluate each platform:

`ACCESS → LOCAL/PROFESSIONAL AUDIENCE FIT → TRUST/PROOF FORMAT FIT → ORGANIC CAPABILITY → PAID CAPABILITY → LEGAL/COMPLIANCE → BOOKING/LEAD HANDOFF → MEASUREMENT → PRODUCTION COST → FRESHNESS`.

Examples of different needs:
- local beauty may need visual/local discovery + simple booking;
- tutor may need expertise, proof, messaging/consultation path;
- B2B consulting may need decision-maker reach, expert content and sales handoff.

No “best social network for services” universal answer.

Output: **Russia Platform Portfolio + rejects**.

## 9. Step 7 — content jobs by service archetype

### S1 local recurring
Possible jobs:
- show result;
- show consistency/service standard;
- reduce local trust risk;
- show provider/personality without lifestyle filler;
- prompt booking/repeat;
- explain care/preparation where useful.

### S2 expert/professional
Possible jobs:
- diagnose problem;
- demonstrate judgment/method;
- explain trade-offs;
- prove fit/outcomes honestly;
- reduce buyer uncertainty;
- support consultation/trial.

### S3 complex B2B service
Possible jobs:
- frame business problem;
- show category expertise;
- prove implementation capability;
- cases/outcomes with context;
- address stakeholder risks;
- enable sales conversations.

Content must show **why this provider/service can be trusted for this type of decision**, not simply maintain posting frequency.

Output: **Service Content Jobs Portfolio**.

## 10. Step 8 — conversion path

### Local recurring
`social → booking/message/phone → visit → repeat/referral`

### Expert/professional
`social → diagnosis/trust → consultation/trial/application → purchase → result/reputation/referral`

### Complex B2B
`social/content exposure → qualified interest/account engagement → discovery → sales/solution process → proposal → contract → expansion/advocacy`

If conversion path is missing, engagement metrics cannot substitute for it.

Output: **Conversion & Handoff Design**.

## 11. Step 9 — measurement

### S1 local recurring
Prioritize:
- booked appointments;
- show-up rate;
- new vs repeat client mix;
- capacity utilization;
- revenue/margin;
- cost per served client;
- local reach quality.

### S2 expert/professional
Prioritize:
- qualified consultation/application;
- consultation→sale conversion;
- client quality/fit;
- retention/renewal where applicable;
- source quality;
- capacity/economics;
- reputation/referral signals where supportable.

### S3 complex B2B
Prioritize:
- relevant account/buying-group exposure;
- qualified conversations;
- influenced pipeline/opportunities;
- sales-cycle movement;
- win/revenue evidence with attribution limitations;
- quality of demand, not raw lead count.

Output: **SMM Measurement Card**.

## 12. Step 10 — STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE

### STOP
- SMM adds little to buyer decision relative to other channels;
- audience/platform mismatch persists;
- economics/capacity make further acquisition harmful;
- legal/operational constraint makes execution unsuitable.

### FIX
- proof weak;
- expertise not legible;
- content attracts wrong clients;
- booking/handoff broken;
- capacity/offer mismatch;
- measurement cannot connect to service outcomes.

### TEST
- new proof/content route;
- alternate platform role;
- consultation/trial path;
- different buyer-role message;
- different organic/paid/creator/community mix.

### SCALE
Only when:
- client quality stays acceptable;
- delivery capacity exists;
- economics remain healthy;
- service quality is not degraded by demand growth;
- business outcome evidence is strong enough.

## 13. Reader completion test for SERVICES

Reader must be able to answer:

1. What service buying pattern do I have?
2. Who pays, uses, influences and decides?
3. What uncertainty must SMM reduce?
4. What is my real geography/capacity constraint?
5. What role should SMM play in the buying process?
6. Which Russia-current platforms fit and why?
7. What must content prove/do?
8. Where does attention convert?
9. What do I measure?
10. What makes me STOP, FIX, TEST or SCALE?

If reader still receives “показывайте экспертность и доверие”, `PRACTICAL_VALUE = REWORK`.

## 14. World evidence / author synthesis gates

Before finalizing, reconcile with:

- persuasion/trust research where materially relevant;
- B2B buying/brand evidence for S3;
- current Russia service-platform usage;

- author synthesis explaining where generic world advice fails for Russian execution.

## 15. No-padding consequence

Do not repeat shared SMM theory for each service archetype.

Evergreen mechanisms are explained once. Service playbooks contain only meaningful deltas in:
- trust/proof;
- geography/capacity;
- buyer roles;
- content jobs;
- conversion;
- measurement;
- economics;
- platform fit.

Same advice with different professions = `MERGE | DELETE | REWORK`.
