## 2. Reader — working definition

Primary reader is not defined by job title but by a concrete responsibility:

**a Russian business owner, self-employed professional, in-house marketer or responsible specialist who must decide how to use SMM to promote a specific product or service and needs a professional operating system rather than disconnected tips.**

The book must remain usable across materially different offer/sales archetypes by branching the decision path, not by flattening them into one generic reader.

Reader subcontexts to test in research:

- owner/marketer of mass or frequently purchased consumer goods;
- owner/marketer of considered/durable goods;
- B2B marketer/sales-linked marketer for complex/high-ticket products;
- local service provider/business;
- expert/professional service provider/business;
- agency/freelance SMM specialist who needs a system to diagnose a client's business rather than just produce content.

## 3. Central reader question

**Как профессионально продвигать конкретный товар или услугу через SMM в российской действительности?**

A valid final book must answer this question for materially different product/service/sales archetypes and show the reader how to choose rather than merely enumerate options.

## 4. Real problem

The reader is surrounded by platform tips, content formulas, AI-generated calendars, short courses and tactical advice, but still lacks a reliable way to decide:

- whether SMM is economically important for this business at all;
- which social platforms deserve attention;
- what role each platform should play in the buying process;
- what content is required for this specific offer and customer journey;
- how organic, paid, creator/community and sales handoff should combine;
- how a mass product differs from a complex B2B product;
- how a local service differs from an expert service;
- what should be measured;
- what to stop, fix, test or scale;
- how to use AI without making the brand/content interchangeable;
- how to operate within current Russian platform and legal constraints.

## 5. Central promise — DRAFT HYPOTHESIS

The book gives the reader a **decision system for building a professional SMM operating model for a specific product or service**: from business objective and offer archetype through platform choice, content jobs, distribution, conversion path and measurement to a repeatable `STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE` management loop.

This promise remains DRAFT until benchmark/AI-substitution/archetype research proves it is sufficiently differentiated and complete.

## 6. Central mechanism / causal model — DRAFT HYPOTHESIS

SMM performance is not caused by “posting more” or “being on the right social network”. The working causal chain is:

`BUSINESS ECONOMICS / GOAL → BUYER & BUYING PROCESS → OFFER / SALES ARCHETYPE → ROLE OF SOCIAL IN THE JOURNEY → PLATFORM / AUDIENCE FIT → TRUST & CONTENT JOBS → DISTRIBUTION MIX → CONVERSION / HANDOFF → MEASUREMENT → DIAGNOSIS → ITERATION`.

Platform tactics are downstream of the earlier decisions.

The hypothesis must be tested against world-class marketing/social/B2B/services evidence. BOOK OS must not present it as an invented scientific law.

## 7. Original contribution hypothesis

The contribution is **not a claim to have invented SMM theory**.

Potential contribution to prove:

1. synthesize strong global marketing/social-media evidence into one coherent decision system;
2. explicitly separate goods/services and materially different sales archetypes instead of giving one universal social playbook;
3. translate the system into the current Russian platform/legal/measurement reality;
4. make every major concept end in a reader decision, action and observable check;
5. expose disagreements/limits in world schools and provide an explicit Elena Dym author position rather than anonymous compilation;
6. separate evergreen mechanisms from time-sensitive platform execution so the book does not become obsolete as a UI manual.

If research shows this synthesis is not materially stronger than existing books/free resources/current AI answers, Definition requires REWORK.

## 8. Reader before → after

### Before

The reader may have channels/pages/content/ad spend but cannot confidently answer:
- where SMM belongs in the business system;
- which platforms to use;
- what content functions matter;
- how to adapt strategy to the offer type;
- why results are weak;
- what metric should drive the next decision.

### After

The reader can:
- diagnose their offer/sales archetype;
- decide whether and how SMM should contribute;
- select a platform portfolio based on actual Russia availability/audience/business fit;
- design a content and proof system appropriate to the buyer journey;
- choose organic/paid/creator/community mechanisms with explicit roles;
- create a conversion/sales handoff appropriate to the business;
- define measurement and experiments;
- operate an iterative `STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE` loop;
- re-check time-sensitive platform/legal assumptions instead of treating them as eternal rules.

## 9. Practical end state / course-in-a-book output

By the end, the reader should have built a bounded set of useful artifacts for their own business, not generic worksheets:

1. **SMM Business Role Brief** — why SMM exists for this business and what outcome it must influence;
2. **Offer/Sales Archetype Diagnosis** — product/service and sales-pattern classification;
3. **Buyer / Buying-Unit Map** — who initiates/influences/decides/pays where relevant;
4. **SMM Journey Role Map** — what social must do at each relevant buying stage;
5. **Russia Platform Portfolio** — selected platforms with purpose and explicit reject reasons for the rest;
6. **Trust / Proof Map** — what must be demonstrated for this archetype;
7. **Content Jobs Portfolio** — content functions, not merely topic rubrics;
8. **Distribution Mix** — organic / paid / creator / community roles where applicable;
9. **Conversion & Handoff Design** — action path to lead, appointment, consultation, e-commerce/offline purchase or sales process;
10. **SMM Measurement Card** — signals/outcomes that matter and vanity metrics explicitly deprioritized;
11. **Experiment / Decision Queue** — next tests with `STOP | FIX | TEST | SCALE` criteria;
12. **Freshness Register** — platform/legal facts that require re-check.

Every artifact must prove practical usefulness during Chapter/Whole-book QA; decorative worksheets are prohibited.

## 10. Offer / Sales Archetype scope

Minimum families under research:

### Goods
- FMCG / mass low-involvement product;
- considered/durable consumer product;
- complex/high-ticket B2B product.

### Services
- local recurring service;
- expert/professional service.

Additional archetypes are admitted only when evidence proves materially different buying process, trust/proof burden, conversion path or measurement logic.

The final taxonomy must be simple enough for a reader to self-classify and rich enough to change the playbook.

## 11. Russia application intent

The practical layer must use only currently usable/relevant Russian SMM channels and separately classify:

`ACCESS | ORGANIC USE | PAID PROMOTION | LEGAL/COMPLIANCE | AUDIENCE FIT | ARCHETYPE FIT | MEASUREMENT | FRESHNESS`.

Current research candidates include VK, OK, Dzen, RUTUBE, MAX, Telegram and conditionally other platforms depending on actual access/legal state. Restricted/unstable platforms cannot anchor the practical playbook.

## 12. World-class benchmark intent

The book must be compared against at least:

- current strategic brand-management work (Keller / Swaminathan and related marketing-management lineage);
- Ehrenberg-Bass / Sharp / Romaniuk evidence on growth, mental availability and category buying;
- Binet / Field effectiveness work;
- Quesenberry and Tuten as direct social-media strategy/category benchmarks;
- services marketing (Wirtz/Lovelock lineage) for service-specific differences;
- B2B Institute / Ehrenberg-Bass B2B research for long-cycle/complex B2B;
- high-quality free professional resources;
- best current AI response to the central question.

Benchmark is a floor and anti-copy tool, not a reading-list ornament.

## 13. Author synthesis intent

For major strategic questions use:

`WORLD VIEWS → EVIDENCE → CONFLICT/GAP → ELENA DYM POSITION → RUSSIA TRANSLATION → ARCHETYPE DIFFERENCE → READER ACTION → OBSERVABLE CHECK`.

The author must remain intellectually visible. If a section only summarizes external authors without producing a defensible author conclusion, it is incomplete.

## 14. NOT THIS BOOK — protected boundaries

This book is NOT:

- a platform-interface manual;
- a collection of post ideas/rubrics/templates;
- a “100 hacks” book;
- a book about follower growth as an end in itself;
- a generic personal-brand manual;
- a comprehensive advertising-effectiveness/attribution book — deep territory protected for «Как оценивать эффективность рекламы»;
- a comprehensive quiz/conversion-mechanics book — protected for «Квиз-маркетинг. Как собирать лиды, вовлекать и продавать через тесты»;
- a marketplace operating system;
- a guide to circumventing blocked/restricted platforms;
- an “AI for SMM” book unless AI materially changes the central decision;
- an academic literature review detached from practice.

## 15. AI-substitution test — preliminary
