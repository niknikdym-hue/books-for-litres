## Rule

The final book may explicitly attribute ideas to named authors/researchers where materially useful: e.g. «Byron Sharp and the Ehrenberg-Bass school argue…», «Les Binet and Peter Field show…», «Kevin Lane Keller and Vanitha Swaminathan frame…». Competing views may be presented side by side when that improves the reader's decision.

Attribution must not become name-dropping. A named source is used only when it contributes a theory, empirical finding, model, disagreement, benchmark or decision rule actually used by the book.

The final Literary Master / Publishing Package must include a reader-usable bibliography of sources actually used. Working/rejected sources remain in research provenance but do not automatically enter the reader bibliography.

## A. Marketing / brand / growth foundations — admitted to benchmark set

1. **Philip Kotler, Kevin Lane Keller, Alexander Chernev. _Marketing Management_, 16th ed.** Pearson, 2022 / Global Edition 2024.  
   Function: market/customer analysis, segmentation, positioning, value proposition, marketing-system baseline.  
   Status: `BENCHMARK / FOUNDATIONAL`.

2. **Kevin Lane Keller, Vanitha Swaminathan. _Strategic Brand Management: Building, Measuring, and Managing Brand Equity_, 6th ed.** Pearson, published 2026.

   Status: `BENCHMARK / CURRENT WORLD-CLASS`.

3. **Byron Sharp. _How Brands Grow: What Marketers Don't Know_.** Oxford University Press, 2010; research lineage: Ehrenberg-Bass Institute.  
   Function: penetration/growth, mental and physical availability, evidence-based challenge to loyalty-heavy assumptions.  
   Status: `BENCHMARK / EMPIRICAL SCHOOL`.

4. **Jenni Romaniuk. _Building Distinctive Brand Assets_ / _Better Brand Health_.** Ehrenberg-Bass Institute / Oxford University Press lineage.

   Status: `BENCHMARK / EMPIRICAL SCHOOL`.

5. **Les Binet, Peter Field. _The Long and the Short of It_.** IPA, 2013; subsequent IPA effectiveness work.  
   Function: long-term brand building vs short-term activation; warning against over-reading short-term digital metrics.  
   Status: `BENCHMARK / EFFECTIVENESS EVIDENCE`.

## F. Bibliography discipline

For every material source used in manuscript-level argument, research provenance must record at least:

- full author/organization/title;
- edition/year when applicable;
- URL/DOI/ISBN where available;
- retrieval/check date for online/time-sensitive sources;
- exact claim/function supported;
- what the source does **not** prove;
- whether the manuscript attributes the source by name;
- whether the source belongs in final reader bibliography.

Quotes are optional and should be rare. Prefer accurate paraphrase with attribution. Do not fabricate page numbers, quotations, case results or author positions.
