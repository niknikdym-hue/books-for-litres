# RESEARCH MAP — «Как продавать услуги»

**Серия:** «Секреты продвижения услуг», книга №1  
**Статус:** RESEARCH / OPEN QUESTIONS  
**Дата:** 2026-09-06  
**Цель:** до архитектуры доказать ключевые механизмы книги и заранее отделить evergreen evidence от быстро устаревающих рыночных наблюдений.

## 1. Правило работы с evidence

Исследование включается в книгу только если выполняет конкретную интеллектуальную функцию.

Не использовать источник:

- «для солидности»;
- ради красивой цифры;
- если он доказывает уже закрытый вывод другой главы;
- если результат нельзя корректно перенести на контекст книги;
- если коммерческий отчёт подаётся как нейтральная научная истина;
- если time-sensitive факт успел устареть к моменту финального master.

Каждому существенному утверждению присваивается класс:

- `EVERGREEN / SUPPORT REQUIRED`;
- `TIME-SENSITIVE`;
- `MARKET DATA`;
- `LEGAL/REGULATORY`;
- `PLATFORM-SPECIFIC`;
- `AUTHOR OBSERVATION`;
- `HYPOTHESIS`.

## 2. Центральная гипотеза книги

### H1

**Профессиональную/сложную услугу труднее оценить до покупки, а иногда и после неё; эта неопределённость повышает perceived risk и заставляет покупателя использовать замещающие сигналы качества.**

Статус: `EVERGREEN / STRONGLY SUPPORTED`.

Primary anchors:

1. `Purchasing professional services: A transaction cost view...` — Industrial Marketing Management.  
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019850114000467  
   Функция: professional services abstract/intangible, difficult to specify/compare/evaluate, perceived as risky; trust-based purchasing used to manage risk.

2. `Risk Perception and Reduction in the Purchase of Consumer Services` — The Service Industries Journal.  
   https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02642069300000068  
   Функция: services perceived as riskier than products; uncertainty is a major driver.

3. `An examination of perceived risk... in search, experience and credence services`.  
   https://openurl.ebsco.com/contentitem/doi%3A10.1108%2F08876049910273763  
   Функция: perceived risk increases along search → experience → credence continuum.

4. `Customer value creation in professional service relationships: the case of credence goods`.  
   https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02642060801990361  
   Функция: professional services with credence qualities involve information asymmetry and perceived risk.

Architecture implication: книга может строиться не вокруг generic «этапов продаж», а вокруг разных причин неопределённости и способов сделать выбор проверяемее.

## 3. Research question R1 — что именно делает услугу трудной для оценки

Нужно различить, не смешивая в одну главу:

- физическую нематериальность;
- mental intangibility — покупатель не может ясно представить, что получит;
- variability / heterogeneity;
- co-production — результат зависит и от участия клиента;
- credence quality — качество трудно оценить даже после;
- outcome uncertainty — специалист может работать хорошо, но внешний результат не полностью контролируется;
- information asymmetry;
- irreversible / costly consequences ошибки выбора.

Supporting anchors:

- Laroche et al., `Exploring How Intangibility Affects Perceived Risk`  
  https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1094670503262955
- `Internet versus bricks-and-mortar retailers: An investigation into intangibility...`  
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022435905000655
- professional-services purchasing paper above.

Status: `OPEN FOR SYNTHESIS`.

Do not prematurely convert this list into chapters.

## 4. R2 — какие quality signals покупатель использует вместо прямой проверки результата

Нужно исследовать роли:

- reputation;
- specialization / visible expertise;
- prior experience;
- referrals;
- case evidence;
- process transparency;
- credentials where relevant;
- specificity of diagnosis/proposal;
- consistency of visible signals;
- brand familiarity;
- price as possible signal (requires careful separate evidence);
- guarantees / warranties / reversibility where legally and commercially appropriate.

Anchors:

1. `Customer perceived value in B-t-B service relationships: ... corporate reputation`  
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019850107001162  
   Function: reputation can operate as shorthand when intrinsic service value is hard to assess.

2. `Exploring the consequences of brand credibility in services`  
   https://scholars.uky.edu/en/publications/exploring-the-consequences-of-brand-credibility-in-services/  
   Function: credibility affects quality perception, risk and purchase intention.

3. `The cues that matter: Screening for quality signals in the ex ante phase of buying professional services`  
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0148296319300955  
   Function: ex-ante quality signals in professional-service selection.

4. Hinge `Inside the Buyer's Brain` research  
   https://hingemarketing.com/library/article/inside-the-buyers-brain-second-edition-executive-summary  
   Function: buyer/seller perception mismatch, reputation/expertise/evaluation context. Commercial research; use with source caveat.

Status: `OPEN / STRONG BASE`.

## 5. R3 — может ли видимость процесса снижать риск

Hypothesis: покупатель легче решается на сложную услугу, когда понимает не только обещанный финал, но и **как** будет устроена работа, какие точки контроля, ответственность и участие сторон.

Anchor:

`The impact of backstage cues on service evaluation`  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/abs/pii/S1756669X17000047

Reported function: backstage visibility reduced pre-purchase perceived risk; visual+text cues outperformed text-only in the studied setting.

Status: `SUPPORTED IN SPECIFIC CONTEXT / GENERALIZATION MUST BE CAREFUL`.

Architecture use: evidence for making the delivery process observable; do not overclaim universal effect size.

## 6. R4 — trust: самостоятельная причина покупки или итог набора сигналов

Книга не должна использовать слово «доверие» как объяснение самого себя.

Нужно разложить trust на наблюдаемые основания:

- competence;
- predictability;
- integrity / honesty about limits;
- alignment of incentives;
- evidence;
- process clarity;
- social proof / reputation;
- prior experience.

Anchor:

`Initiation of buyer–seller relationships: The impact of intangibility, trust and mitigation strategies`  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019850114001837

Function: risk induced by intangibility creates a threshold of trust; sellers can reduce intangibility or enhance trust.

Status: `SUPPORTED / REQUIRES CONCEPTUAL CLEANUP`.

Editorial constraint: не писать главы «Как вызвать доверие» без операционального разложения.

## 7. R5 — специализация и visible expertise

Hypothesis: в сложных professional services покупателю важнее доказуемая релевантная экспертиза по его проблеме, чем абстрактная универсальность.

Sources:

- Hinge on expertise as differentiation  
  https://hingemarketing.com/blog/story/expertise-as-a-differentiation-strategy
- Hinge referral research  
  https://hingemarketing.com/blog/story/new-research-report-referral-marketing-for-professional-services-firms

Useful reported findings include expertise-based and reputation-based referrals and the role of visible expertise.

Status: `COMMERCIAL RESEARCH / SUPPORTIVE, NOT SOLE EVIDENCE`.

Need additional neutral evidence if this becomes a central chapter claim.

## 8. R6 — referrals: почему рекомендация не завершает продажу

Question: после рекомендации покупатель всё равно проверяет поставщика; referral может открыть дверь, но не устранить uncertainty.

Hinge sources:

- https://hingemarketing.com/landing-pages/referral-marketing-rain-group
- https://hingemarketing.com/blog/story/rethinking-referral-marketing-a-new-research-based-approach-to-referrals

Reported research: non-client referrals are common; referred firms can still be ruled out before direct contact.

Status: `COMMERCIAL RESEARCH / USE FOR MECHANISM, NOT UNIVERSAL NUMBERS WITHOUT RECHECK`.

Potential reader output: audit what happens **after** recommendation.

## 9. R7 — B2B self-directed research and hidden buyers

For B2B professional services, the person speaking to the seller may not be the only evaluator.

Edelman–LinkedIn 2025:
https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report

Research frame:

- buying groups include hidden decision-makers;
- internal alignment can stall deals;
- high-quality thought leadership can influence unseen participants.

Status: `TIME-SENSITIVE B2B EVIDENCE`.

Scope constraint: this is relevant only to B2B chapters/examples; do not universalize to every solo B2C service.

## 11. R9 — discovery / diagnosis before proposal

Hypothesis: quality of service sale depends on correct problem definition because professional services often require provider participation in diagnosing the need; a premature proposal creates solution mismatch and weakens perceived relevance.

Anchors:

- credence professional-services research;
- `The cues that matter...` on importance of ex-ante phase;
- professional-services purchasing literature.

Status: `SUPPORTED CONCEPTUALLY / NEED SALES-SPECIFIC EVIDENCE`.

Research task before chapter contract: distinguish true diagnostic conversation from manipulative questioning scripts.

## 12. R10 — qualification: not every lead should buy

Need evidence and expert practice around:

- problem/provider fit;
- ability/willingness to participate;
- budget vs expected scope;
- timing;
- decision authority in B2B;
- risk of accepting bad-fit clients for service quality and reputation.

Status: `OPEN`.

Editorial rule: no invented universal scoring formula.

## 13. R11 — proposal design

Research questions:

- what information reduces evaluation difficulty;
- role of options vs one proposal;
- clarity of scope and exclusions;
- evidence placement;
- process/timeline visibility;
- responsibilities of client/provider;
- decision friction caused by proposal complexity.

Status: `OPEN`.

Potential evidence classes: B2B proposal research, behavioral decision research, professional-services ex-ante quality signals.

## 14. R12 — price, perceived risk and value

This block requires exceptional care because «цена = ценность» is an anti-junk trap.

Separate questions:

1. When does high price act as a quality signal?
2. When does it simply increase financial risk?
3. What does buyer compare price against: alternative, outcome, internal cost, competitor, doing nothing?
4. How should scope uncertainty be priced?
5. Fixed fee / hourly / retainer / outcome-linked — what risk is transferred to which side?
6. How does AI affect client willingness to pay for time-based work?

Status: `OPEN / HIGH PRIORITY`.

Time-sensitive context:

- 2026 fee pressure in audit/consulting tied to AI efficiency expectations;
- current discussion of fixed/performance-based models.

Use journalistic sources only as current context, never as universal proof.

## 16. R14 — objections as categories of uncertainty

Proposed distinction to research:

- `VALUE / OUTCOME uncertainty`;
- `PROVIDER uncertainty`;
- `PROCESS uncertainty`;
- `PRICE / FINANCIAL risk`;
- `TIMING risk`;
- `FIT uncertainty`;
- `INTERNAL DECISION uncertainty`;
- `STATUS QUO / change risk`.

Status: `HYPOTHESIS / ARCHITECTURAL CANDIDATE`.

Need external research before treating taxonomy as fact. It may remain an author-created practical diagnostic if explicitly framed as such.

## 17. R15 — guarantees, trials, pilots, staged commitments

Question: which risk-reversal mechanisms legitimately reduce purchase risk without creating false promises or unmanageable provider risk?

Candidates:

- paid diagnostic;
- pilot / limited scope;
- phased project;
- milestone review;
- transparent cancellation/exit conditions;
- bounded guarantee where outcome is controllable;
- sample / demonstration / audit.

Status: `OPEN`.

Legal/ethical gate required for claims about guarantees.

## 18. R16 — post-sale expectation alignment as part of selling

Hypothesis: sale quality is not complete at payment; mismatched expectations create future service failure, refund conflict and reputation damage.

Need research on:

- expectation confirmation;
- service quality gaps;
- role of onboarding/contract clarity.

Status: `OPEN / MAY BE SMALL FINAL SECTION, NOT CLIENT-SERVICE BOOK`.

## 19. R17 — buyer's cost of doing nothing

Common sales literature heavily uses «cost of inaction». Need establish when it is legitimate and when it becomes manipulative urgency.

Research questions:

- how buyers evaluate status quo risk;
- omission bias / inertia / switching costs;
- ethical framing of consequences.

Status: `OPEN`.

## 20. R18 — proof: what counts and what does not

Need a hierarchy / diagnostic, but do not invent one as scientific fact.

Possible evidence types:

- verifiable outcome data;
- before/after with context;
- specific case narrative;
- client testimonial;
- independent review;
- credential/licence;
- demonstration/sample;
- published expertise;
- transparent method/process;
- quantified operating evidence.

Research task: distinguish **relevance, verifiability and comparability**.

Status: `OPEN / HIGH PRIORITY`.

## 21. R19 — ethics of selling credence services

Especially important where buyer cannot easily evaluate necessity/quality even after purchase.

Questions:

- information asymmetry and opportunity for over-treatment / over-selling;
- professional ethics;
- promises beyond provider control;
- conflicts of interest.

Status: `EVERGREEN / HIGH IMPORTANCE`.

This protects the book from teaching «risk reduction» as persuasion at any cost.

## 22. Evidence hierarchy for the manuscript

Preferred order for factual claims:

1. peer-reviewed research / recognized academic synthesis;
2. primary official data / regulator / original research report;
3. high-quality large-sample professional research with transparent methodology;
4. reputable business journalism for current market developments;
5. expert practice / case evidence;
6. author observation, clearly labelled;
7. model/hypothesis, clearly labelled.

Never convert level 4–7 evidence into a universal scientific law.

## 24. Research gate before Architecture

До построения Architecture v1 достаточно доказать:

- центральный mechanism H1 — PASS;
- существование нескольких разных uncertainty/risk sources — PASS / needs synthesis;
- роль signals/reputation/process visibility — PASS / context-sensitive;
- AI-era market shift — PASS as time-sensitive context;
- category gap — PASS.

Открытые R9–R19 не блокируют саму архитектуру, но **блокируют chapter contract**, если конкретная глава будет зависеть от недоказанного утверждения.

## 25. Запрет на research overlap

Когда конкретный источник или research function закрепляется за главой в Architecture:

- он регистрируется в `UNIQUENESS-LEDGER.md`;
- соседняя глава не использует новый источник, чтобы повторить тот же вывод;
- research evidence не должно превращать книгу в обзор литературы;
- один и тот же факт не повторяется во вступлении, основной главе и финале ради «усиления».
