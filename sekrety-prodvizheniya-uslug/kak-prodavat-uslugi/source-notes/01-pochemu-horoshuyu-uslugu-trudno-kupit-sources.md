# SOURCE NOTES — Chapter 1 «Почему хорошую услугу трудно купить»

**Статус:** DRAFT EVIDENCE NOTES  
**Дата:** 2026-09-07

Эти notes поддерживают factual claims главы и не являются частью literary prose.

## 1. Professional services are difficult to specify/evaluate and involve risk

`Purchasing professional services: A transaction cost view of the antecedents and consequences of purchasing formalization` — Industrial Marketing Management.  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019850114000467

Function: professional services can be abstract/intangible and difficult to specify, compare and evaluate; buyer uses mechanisms including trust/formalization to manage uncertainty/risk.

Use in Ch1: foundation for `hard-to-evaluate service → uncertainty/risk`.

Do not infer: every service is equally risky or every buyer behaves the same way.

## 2. Services and perceived risk

`Risk Perception and Reduction in the Purchase of Consumer Services` — The Service Industries Journal.  
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02642069300000068

Function: supports service-purchase risk/uncertainty logic.

Use: general mechanism only.

## 3. Search / experience / credence and information asymmetry

`An examination of perceived risk, information search and behavioral intentions in search, experience and credence services`.  
https://openurl.ebsco.com/contentitem/doi%3A10.1108%2F08876049910273763

`Customer value creation in professional service relationships: the case of credence goods`.  
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02642060801990361

Function: support distinctions in evaluability and credence-service information asymmetry.

Use: paragraph explaining why some quality remains difficult to evaluate even after service delivery.

## 4. Intangibility and perceived risk

Laroche et al., `Exploring How Intangibility Affects Perceived Risk`.  
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1094670503262955

Function: support mental/physical intangibility as part of risk formation.

Use: Ch1 mechanism; Ch2 owns practical rewriting of decision object.

## 5. Ex-ante quality signals in professional services

Pemer & Skjølsvik, `The cues that matter: Screening for quality signals in the ex ante phase of buying professional services`, Journal of Business Research (2019).  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0148296319300955

Study: interviews with 51 professional-services clients; develops taxonomy of dimensions/signals used to assess service quality ex ante.

Function: supports buyer use of observable signals when quality itself cannot be directly evaluated pre-purchase.

Use: Ch1 mentions reputation, specialization, prior work, credentials, process cues etc. Ch3 owns detailed Evidence Inventory and must not repeat Ch1 exposition.

## 6. Price as cue vs price as risk

`The Effects of Awareness and Quality Risk on the Price-Quality Relationship Among Services`.  
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1300/J127v01n01_06

Function: supports context dependence of price-quality cue; price is not a universal proxy for quality.

`Price negotiating for services: elucidating the ambivalent effects on customers’ negotiation aspirations`, Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science.  
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11747-019-00676-4

Function: service heterogeneity/quality uncertainty can contribute to perceived product risk and negotiation behavior.

Use in Ch1: only establish that price can become a visible simplifying cue / risk response. Ch8 owns pricing/risk reasoning.

## 7. Multi-stakeholder B2B buying and uncertainty

Gartner, `Help B2B Buyers Navigate Change Management for Complex Purchases` (2024).  
https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/5088931

Gartner 2025 sales survey on conflict within B2B buyer teams.  
https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-05-07-gartner-sales-survey-finds-74-percent-of-b2b-buyer-teams-demonstrate-unhealthy-conflict-during-the-decision-process

Use: contextual support for multi-stakeholder B2B decision uncertainty.

## 9. Author-synthesis boundary

The seven-part working `Buyer Uncertainty Map` in Ch1 is **author synthesis**.

Research supports multiple distinct sources of uncertainty/risk and observable screening signals; it does not establish this exact seven-bucket taxonomy as a validated universal model.

The manuscript must preserve this boundary explicitly.
