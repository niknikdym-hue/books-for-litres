## 1. Customer compatibility is an operational service variable

### Buell, Campbell & Frei — Management Science, 2020/2021
`The Customer May Not Always Be Right: Customer Compatibility and Service Performance`  
https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3596

Definition used by authors:
- customer compatibility = degree of fit between customers’ needs and the capabilities of the operations serving them.

Research frame includes:
- variance-decomposition work on customer satisfaction;
- interaction-level banking and quick-service restaurant data;
- customer compatibility analyses;
- longitudinal U.S. bank branch performance data (2006–2017).

Relevant reported findings:
- customer-level differences account for a substantial portion of explainable satisfaction variance in the studied models;
- customers whose needs diverged more from those typically served by their bank reported lower satisfaction;
- institutions serving more dispersed customer needs showed weaker branch growth in the reported longitudinal analysis.

Use in Ch6:
- supports **fit between customer needs and service-operation capabilities** as a real service-performance variable;
- legitimizes a decision step after task understanding: `can our service operation actually serve this need well?`

Boundaries:
- empirical contexts are banking / quick-service restaurants, not professional-service selling;
- no transfer of numerical effects to consulting/legal/IT/etc.;
- does not validate a sales qualification scorecard;
- does not imply «difficult customers are bad».

## 2. Client involvement in professional services affects delivery under conditions

### Fu, Flood, Rousseau, Morris & Johnstone — PLOS ONE, 2023
`When are clients helpful? Capitalising on client involvement in professional service delivery`  
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0280738

Study frame:
- 58 project managers and 171 consultants nested in professional-service project teams;
- multi-level analysis;
- examines direct and conditional contribution of client involvement to project outcomes.

Reported findings:
- client involvement positively related to team performance and individual idea creativity in the study;
- team bonding capital moderated these relationships;
- impact of client involvement was stronger when team bonding capital was high;
- when team bonding capital was low, client involvement did not improve team performance and inhibited individual idea creativity in the reported analysis.

Important background in the paper:
- professional-service clients may provide knowledge, feedback and problem-solving inputs;
- client involvement can also add complexity or politicize the consulting process;
- clients may disagree on problem definitions or preferred solutions.

Use in Ch6:
- supports client participation as a material condition of professional-service delivery, not a decorative «relationship» variable;
- supports checking whether required participation is feasible and whether the service configuration can actually use client involvement productively.

Boundary:
- professional-services team context;
- no universal rule that «more client involvement is better»;
- do not convert team-bonding findings into a sales score;
- no claim that seller can predict project success perfectly before sale.

## 5. Approved synthesis candidate

### Qualification / No-Go Criteria
Reader-facing Russian name candidate: **Критерии совместимости сделки**.

Proposed fit dimensions:

1. **PROBLEM–CAPABILITY FIT**  
   Is the task within the provider’s real competence/service capability?

2. **OUTCOME / CONTROL FIT**  
   Can the provider make a meaningful controllable commitment, or is the client requiring an outcome mostly outside provider control?

3. **PARTICIPATION / INPUT FIT**  
   Can the client realistically provide the access, data, decisions, people and feedback the service genuinely requires?

4. **SCOPE / TIME / CAPACITY FIT**  
   Can the work be delivered at the required scope/timing with available provider capacity, or must the configuration change?

5. **EXPECTATION FIT**  
   Do parties accept the material boundaries, assumptions and non-guaranteed outcomes already made explicit in Ch2/Ch4?

6. **PROFESSIONAL / ETHICAL / LEGAL FIT**  
   Is the provider actually allowed and professionally competent to take this work; are there conflicts that should block the sale?

Status: **AUTHOR-CREATED SYNTHESIS**, not a validated scientific qualification scale.

No numeric score by default. One critical no-go condition can outweigh several positive fields.

## 6. Decision outputs

Ch6 should yield three primary actions:

1. **PROCEED** — configuration is sufficiently workable;
2. **REDESIGN** — task may be worth doing, but scope/roles/timing/conditions need to change before offer;
3. **DECLINE / REFER** — provider should not sell this configuration; referral/alternative can be offered where appropriate.

Do not use `CLARIFY` as a main output because information clarification is owned by Ch5.

## 7. Economic / practical function

Ch6 protects against:
- revenue that creates delivery loss;
- predictable scope/participation failure;
- selling outside competence;
- impossible external-outcome expectations;
- capacity overcommitment;
- accepting a configuration likely to create expectation conflict and reputation damage.

It is not a moral chapter about «bad clients».

Core idea:
> bad fit is often a property of a **configuration**, not a person.

A client can be a poor fit for one scope/timing/service operation and a good fit after redesign or for another provider.

## 8. Russia application

No universal «Russian customer» traits.

High-value contexts:
- self-employed specialist / NPD: personal capacity is narrow, so one bad-fit project can crowd out several good projects;
- ИП / small agency: unclear client inputs or impossible timing can become direct margin/capacity risk;
- regulated professional services: competence/licensing/conflict/ethical boundaries may create hard no-go criteria;
- B2B: decision authority/implementation owner matters only insofar as it affects actual participation and delivery.

Any specific regulatory/licensing/legal example must be fresh-checked against official Russian sources before master.
