## 1. Backstage visibility / pre-purchase risk

### Liu, Xu & Ling — International Journal of Quality and Service Sciences, 2017
`The impact of backstage cues on service evaluation`  
https://doi.org/10.1108/IJQSS-04-2016-0024

Study frame:
- two experimental studies;
- investigates backstage visibility at pre-purchase and post-purchase stages;
- Study 1 reported lower pre-purchase perceived risk among customers exposed to backstage cues;
- pictures + text outperformed text-only in the studied design;
- risk-reduction effect stronger for high-credence than low-credence services;
- perceived intangibility partially mediated the pre-purchase effect.

Use in chapter:
- selective visibility of otherwise hidden service activity can make a service more mentally observable and reduce part of pre-purchase risk.

Do NOT claim:
- a universal conversion lift;
- that every service should reveal all backstage activity;
- exact effect size in Russian professional services;
- that visual process maps are always superior to text in every context.

## 2. KIBS participation / role clarity

### `Are knowledge intensive business services really co-produced? Overcoming lack of customer participation in KIBS`
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019850115001352

Use:
- customers may participate in service specification, solution choice and solving delivery complications;
- low expertise, role clarity or available resources can inhibit active participation;
- supports making client inputs/role visible before purchase.

Boundary:
- knowledge-intensive B2B context;
- not universal proof for every B2C service.

### Dong & Sivakumar — Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science, 2017
`Customer participation in services: domain, scope, and boundaries`  
https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/joamsc/v45y2017i6d10.1007_s11747-017-0524-y.html

Use:
- participation is heterogeneous; proposed types include mandatory, replaceable and voluntary participation;
- supports chapter decision not to use one generic «клиент участвует» rule.

Do not import their typology as our chapter framework unless needed and directly cited.

### Chen, Raab & Tanford — International Journal of Hospitality Management, 2015
`Antecedents of mandatory customer participation in service encounters: An empirical study`  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278431915000195

Use:
- role clarity is one studied antecedent of customer participation in hospitality service encounters;
- supports practical importance of telling customers what is expected from them.

Boundary:
- hospitality context;
- no direct effect-size transfer to consulting/IT/legal services.
