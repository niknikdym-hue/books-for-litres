## BUSINESS PROBLEM

Коммерческое предложение часто строится из того, что удобно показать продавцу: рассказ о компании, перечень услуг, длинный состав работ, кейсы, цена, общие преимущества. Покупателю приходится самостоятельно восстанавливать связь между своей ситуацией, предлагаемым обязательством, доказательствами, будущим процессом, условиями сделки и следующим решением.

Экономический ущерб:

- decision-maker, не присутствовавший на разговоре, не понимает логику предложения;
- предложения выглядят взаимозаменяемыми и упрощаются до цены;
- важные границы/условия обнаруживаются после согласия;
- объём информации растёт, но decision clarity не растёт;
- seller proof/process details используются без связи с конкретным buyer decision.

## UNIQUE QUESTION

**Как собрать уже выясненные элементы продажи в коммерческий документ, по которому покупатель способен восстановить логику решения без повторной консультации продавца?**

Ch7 не создаёт заново содержимое предыдущих глав. Она владеет **integration / decision-document function**.

## MECHANISM / CAUSAL CHAIN

`seller-centric or incomplete proposal → buyer must reconstruct problem–solution–provider–delivery–terms logic from fragments → higher cognitive/decision effort + unresolved gaps → price-only simplification / internal approval friction / no-decision`.

Counter-mechanism:

`decision-relevant information + sufficient quality + context-appropriate organization → buyer can reconstruct the purchase decision with less seller-dependent interpretation`.

No claim that one page order universally maximizes conversion.

## DECISION

For one real opportunity, seller decides:

1. which information is materially necessary for this buyer to decide;
2. which already-created elements from Ch2–6 must appear;
3. which seller-centered information can be removed;
4. what can be stated briefly vs needs detail;
5. what remains a separate economic decision for Ch8;
6. what exact next decision/action buyer must take.

## ACTION / ARTIFACT

### Offer Decision Sheet / Карта решения по предложению

This is an **AUTHOR-CREATED SYNTHESIS / audit tool**, not a mandatory proposal template.

Eight decision fields:

1. **СИТУАЦИЯ / ЗАДАЧА**  
   What client situation is this proposal responding to? Use Ch5 output without reteaching diagnosis.

2. **ЧТО ПРЕДЛАГАЕТСЯ КУПИТЬ**  
   Work result / service decision object, material scope boundaries, exclusions/assumptions. Use Ch2.

3. **ОСНОВАНИЯ ВЫБРАТЬ ЭТОГО ИСПОЛНИТЕЛЯ**  
   Only relevant proof for this decision. Use Ch3; no proof catalogue.

4. **КАК БУДЕТ УСТРОЕНА РАБОТА**  
   Decision-relevant stages, client role, checkpoints/change rules. Use Ch4; no PM manual.

5. **КРИТИЧЕСКИЕ УСЛОВИЯ СОВМЕСТИМОСТИ**  
   Only conditions materially required for delivery after Ch6; no re-qualification checklist.

6. **ИНВЕСТИЦИЯ / УСЛОВИЯ ОПЛАТЫ**  
   Amount and agreed/basic terms visible. Economic design/reasoning belongs to Ch8.

7. **ЧТО ОСТАЁТСЯ НЕ ГАРАНТИРОВАНО / ЗАВИСИТ ОТ УСЛОВИЙ**  
   Residual risk/limits where materially relevant; no legalistic disclaimer dump.

8. **СЛЕДУЮЩЕЕ РЕШЕНИЕ / ДЕЙСТВИЕ**  
   What buyer needs to decide/do next; no fake urgency.

No universal mandatory order. Actual proposal form and sequence depend on context.

## OBSERVABLE CHECK

### Buyer-retell test

Give the proposal to a relevant person who did not attend the sales conversation. Without additional seller explanation, they should be able to state:

- what situation/problem the offer addresses;
- what exactly is being purchased;
- material scope/boundaries;
- why this provider is plausibly capable;
- how work will unfold at a meaningful level;
- what the client must do;
- how much / under what basic payment terms;
- what material outcome/risk is not guaranteed;
- what decision/action is required next.

If a basic field can only be reconstructed by calling the seller, proposal has a decision-information gap.

Fail conditions:

- document is longer but not more decision-complete;
- proof appears because it is impressive, not relevant;
- detailed process hides the actual decision;
- pricing theory/discount logic leaks from Ch8;
- proposal repeats Ch5 discovery questions;
- seller biography/company history dominates buyer problem;
- legal/disclaimer volume replaces commercial clarity.

## EVIDENCE FUNCTION

### Pemer & Skjølsvik — Journal of Business Research, 2019
`The cues that matter: Screening for quality signals in the ex ante phase of buying professional services`  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2019.02.005

Use:
- interviews with 51 clients of professional services;
- ex-ante quality is difficult to assess under information asymmetry;
- clients use multiple quality dimensions/signals during screening.

Boundary:
- Ch3 owns detailed signal/proof logic;
- Ch7 only integrates relevant evidence into the decision document;
- no proposal-layout conversion claim.

### Dawes, Dowling & Patterson — Industrial Marketing Management, 1992
`Criteria used to select management consultants`  
https://doi.org/10.1016/0019-8501(92)90015-L

Use:
- survey of 253 organizations and 17 selection criteria;
- supports multi-criteria nature of final professional-service selection.

Boundary:
- old management-consultancy context;
- no universal ranking or section list imported.

### Watt, Kayis & Willey — International Journal of Project Management, 2009
`Identifying key factors in the evaluation of tenders for projects and services`  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijproman.2008.03.002

Use:
- contractor/service tender selection as multi-attribute decision;
- delivery/technical solution can matter alongside capability, experience, reputation and cost.

Boundary:
- formal project/tender procurement context only;
- no universal КП structure.

### Keller & Staelin — Journal of Consumer Research, 1987
`Effects of Quality and Quantity of Information on Decision Effectiveness`  
https://doi.org/10.1086/209106

Use:
- experimental findings compatible with distinction between information quality and quantity;
- more information is not automatically more effective.

Boundary:
- consumer/product context;
- no proposal page-count or service conversion claim.

### Schkade & Kleinmuntz — Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes, 1994
`Information Displays and Choice Processes: Differential Effects of Organization, Form, and Sequence`  
https://doi.org/10.1006/obhd.1994.1018

Use:
- organization/form/sequence of information influenced decision processes/effort in studied multi-attribute choices;
- supports proposal-as-decision-aid framing.

Boundary:
- loan-application experimental context;
- no validated proposal ordering.

## RUSSIA APPLICATION

No universal claim that every Russian service needs a formal `КП`.

### Owner-led / ordinary B2B
Proposal may be a short PDF, document, email or structured message if the decision is simple. The audit checks decision completeness, not file format.

### Formal B2B / procurement
Buyer may prescribe tender/procurement fields. Offer Decision Sheet works as an internal completeness audit and does not replace procurement requirements.

### B2C / individual service
Decision information may be distributed across website, message, offer/contract and booking flow rather than one `КП`. Any mandatory pre-contract information claim requires fresh official Russian source verification before master.

Do not state that КП itself is a legal contract.

## SCENE / CASE

Model/composite:

A decision-maker receives two proposals after the same discovery meeting.

Proposal A: 28 slides — company history, awards, service catalogue, team photos, methodology diagram, many cases, price at the end.

Proposal B: shorter but decision-oriented — current situation, exact work result/scope, 2 relevant proof points, meaningful delivery stages, material client input, investment/terms and next decision.

Function: **decision reconstruction burden**, not aesthetics/minimalism.

Important: do not imply «shorter is always better». A complex procurement may legitimately require more detail.
