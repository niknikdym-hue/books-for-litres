## UNIQUE QUESTION
Какие observable signals помогают buyer оценить quality/relevance ex ante и как связать evidence с конкретным uncertainty?

## MECHANISM
`unobservable quality → screening by signals → useful proof depends on relevance + verifiability + what it actually supports`.
