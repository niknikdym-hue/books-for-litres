## BUSINESS PROBLEM

После хорошей диагностики продавец понимает задачу, но по инерции считает, что следующий профессиональный шаг — обязательно сделать предложение. Для услуг это опасно: результат зависит не только от качества специалиста, но и от соответствия задачи его реальным возможностям, объёма/сроков, доступности клиентских входов и допустимых профессиональных границ.

Экономический ущерб bad-fit sale:

- занятая capacity, которая вытесняет подходящие проекты;
- delivery failure, хотя продавец формально «закрыл сделку»;
- scope conflict и внеплановый труд;
- невозможное ожидание внешнего результата;
- репутационный ущерб;
- работа вне фактической компетенции или допустимых профессиональных границ;
- необходимость спасать проект скидками/допработами позже.

## UNIQUE QUESTION

**Стоит ли именно эту задачу продавать именно этому клиенту в этой конфигурации услуги, участия, сроков и ответственности?**

Ch5 уже ответила: `мы достаточно понимаем задачу?`  
Ch6 отвечает: `эта конфигурация достаточно совместима, чтобы её продавать?`

## MECHANISM / CAUSAL CHAIN

`understood task + provider/client/scope/participation mismatch → predictable delivery/expectation risk → sale creates future failure/cost`.

Counter-mechanism:

`explicit compatibility test before offer → proceed / redesign configuration / decline or refer`.

Bad fit is treated primarily as a property of a **configuration**, not a moral label attached to a client.

## DECISION

Three primary outputs:

1. **PROCEED** — the configuration is sufficiently workable to design/issue an offer;
2. **REDESIGN** — the underlying task may be worth doing, but scope, roles, timing or conditions must change first;
3. **DECLINE / REFER** — this provider should not sell the current configuration; referral/alternative can be offered where appropriate.

`CLARIFY` is not a Ch6 outcome: missing-information work belongs to Ch5.

## ACTION / ARTIFACT

### Qualification / No-Go Criteria
Reader-facing name: **Критерии совместимости сделки**.

Six fields:

1. **ЗАДАЧА ↔ ВОЗМОЖНОСТИ ИСПОЛНИТЕЛЯ**  
   Is the problem within real competence/service capability?

2. **ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬСТВО ↔ КОНТРОЛЬ**  
   Can the provider make a meaningful controllable commitment, or is the requested outcome mostly outside provider control?

3. **УЧАСТИЕ / ВХОДЫ КЛИЕНТА**  
   Can the client realistically provide access, data, decisions, people and feedback genuinely required by the service?

4. **ОБЪЁМ / СРОК / CAPACITY**  
   Can the provider deliver the required configuration without predictable overload or structural shortcut?

5. **ОЖИДАНИЯ ↔ ГРАНИЦЫ**  
   Do parties accept material boundaries, assumptions and non-guaranteed outcomes already made explicit?

6. **ПРОФЕССИОНАЛЬНЫЕ / ЭТИЧЕСКИЕ / ПРАВОВЫЕ ГРАНИЦЫ**  
   Is the provider competent/allowed to take the work and free from a blocking conflict where applicable?

Status: **AUTHOR-CREATED SYNTHESIS**, not a validated scientific scorecard.

No default numeric score. A single hard no-go condition may override several positive dimensions.

## OBSERVABLE CHECK

Reader can take one real opportunity and state:

- which compatibility dimensions are sufficiently supported by facts;
- which condition would require redesign before offer;
- whether there is a hard no-go;
- why decision = proceed / redesign / decline;
- what change could turn an incompatible configuration into a workable one, if possible.

Fail conditions:

- criteria are personality judgments (`нравится / не нравится клиент`);
- «химия» replaces observable condition;
- ability to pay is treated as moral quality;
- unclear information from Ch5 is disguised as «bad fit»;
- every risk is solved by asking another question;
- price strategy from Ch8 is imported;
- seller uses qualification to reject any inconvenient client input rather than redesign service.

## EVIDENCE FUNCTION

### Buell, Campbell & Frei — Management Science, 2020/2021
`The Customer May Not Always Be Right: Customer Compatibility and Service Performance`  
https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3596

Use:
- defines customer compatibility as fit between customer needs and capabilities of serving operations;
- empirical work links compatibility differences with satisfaction/performance outcomes in studied banking/QSR contexts;
- supports compatibility as a real operational variable.

Boundary:
- contexts are not professional-service sales;
- no numerical transfer;
- no scientific validation of our qualification map.

### Fu, Flood, Rousseau, Morris & Johnstone — PLOS ONE, 2023
`When are clients helpful? Capitalising on client involvement in professional service delivery`  
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0280738

Use:
- 58 project managers + 171 consultants in professional-service project teams;
- client involvement related to performance/idea creativity in the study;
- effects were conditional on team bonding capital;
- paper discusses both benefits and complications of client involvement.

Boundary:
- more involvement is not automatically better;
- Ch6 checks whether required involvement can realistically work in this service configuration;
- no predictive success score.

Detailed traceability: `../source-notes/06-kogda-ne-prodavat-sources.md`.

## WORLD-CLASS BENCHMARK

### Book Yourself Solid / conventional qualification systems
Benchmark = practical clarity about whom/what to serve.

Do not copy:
- ideal-client personality profiles;
- red-flag lists;
- lead scoring as pseudo-precision;
- «only work with people you love» lifestyle framing.

### Professional-services benchmark
Strong professional judgment includes knowing where expertise/operation can genuinely create value and where commitment is misleading.

### Chapter contribution
Treat qualification as **service compatibility / future-delivery risk**, not sales filtering:

`understood task → compatibility conditions → transaction configuration decision`.

This makes decline one possible professional sales outcome rather than a failure to close.

## RUSSIA APPLICATION

No «Russian clients are...» claims.

High-value contexts:

### Self-employed / NPD specialist
Personal capacity is narrow. One under-scoped / participation-heavy project can crowd out several normal projects. Use capacity as an operational constraint, not a status judgment about the client.

### ИП / small agency
Check whether promised timing, scope and required client inputs are feasible for the actual team.

### Regulated professional services
Licensing, competence, conflict-of-interest, advertising/promise boundaries may create hard no-go conditions. Specific legal examples require fresh official-source review before master.

### B2B
Decision/implementation roles matter only if they affect actual ability to deliver, provide inputs or authorize necessary decisions. Do not import enterprise procurement complexity into simple owner-led deals.

## SCENE / CASE

Model/composite:

A small agency receives an attractive project with good fee and a recognizable client. Task is understood. The project nevertheless assumes impossible launch timing, client data that are not actually accessible and weekly approval from a director who cannot participate. The point is not «bad client». The current **configuration** is bad. A redesigned pilot or narrower first stage may become workable; otherwise decline.

Function: compatibility after diagnosis, not Ch5 information gap.

## COMPOSITION

Tempting revenue opportunity → why a sale can create future operational loss → customer compatibility evidence → professional-service participation conditions → configuration vs person distinction → six compatibility fields → `proceed / redesign / decline` → complication: redesign can save the deal → transition to proposal assembly.

Avoid repeating Ch5 structure. Ch5 moves from uncertainty in problem formulation to information sufficiency. Ch6 moves from an already-understood opportunity to a **commitment decision**.
