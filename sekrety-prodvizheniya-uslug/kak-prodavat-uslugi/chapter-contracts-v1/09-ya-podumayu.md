## BUSINESS PROBLEM
Разные причины no-decision звучат одинаково; seller применяет один persuasion/discount response и отвечает не на реальную причину hesitation.

## UNIQUE QUESTION
Как различить unresolved uncertainty и выбрать подходящее действие — включая решение остановиться?

## MECHANISM
`different unresolved risks → same surface phrase → generic objection handling → mismatch response → continued hesitation/resistance`.

## DECISION
Выбрать: clarify, provide evidence, redesign process/terms, wait, involve stakeholder, decline, or accept genuine no.

## ACTION / ARTIFACT
**Uncertainty/Objection Diagnostic**: `signal → competing hypotheses → verification → appropriate action → stop condition`.

## OBSERVABLE CHECK
Для «дорого/подумаю/не сейчас» reader формулирует несколько hypotheses и проверяет их до действия; не автоматически снижает цену/давит urgency.

## EVIDENCE FUNCTION
Perceived risk; price negotiation/risk reduction; B2B internal decision complexity. Exact taxonomy remains synthesis unless evidence supports it.

## WORLD-CLASS BENCHMARK
Not a classic objection-handling script chapter. Contribution: objection as diagnostic evidence about unresolved purchase decision.

## RUSSIA APPLICATION
Solo B2C vs owner-led B2B vs buying-group B2B; no unsupported cultural stereotypes.

## FRESHNESS
Core evergreen-ish; B2B buying research current-context recheck.

## SCENE / CASE
