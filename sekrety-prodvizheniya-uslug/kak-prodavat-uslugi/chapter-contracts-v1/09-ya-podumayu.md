## BUSINESS PROBLEM

После предложения продавец получает короткий сигнал: «дорого», «не сейчас», «я подумаю», «надо обсудить», тишину после обещания вернуться. Такие сигналы легко превратить в готовую причину. Тогда продавец снижает цену, присылает ещё кейсы, создаёт срочность или запускает очередной follow-up, не проверив, что именно мешает решению.

Коммерческий ущерб:

- economic problem лечится доказательствами или наоборот;
- реальный timing/priority трактуется как objection;
- внутреннее B2B-согласование путается с недоверием к продавцу;
- genuine no бесконечно «обрабатывается»;
- сильный клиент получает давление вместо релевантного следующего шага;
- продавец тратит время на действия, которые не могут изменить причину остановки.

## UNIQUE QUESTION

**Как после конкретного предложения отличить наблюдаемый сигнал от его возможной причины и выбрать следующий ход только после проверки конкурирующих гипотез?**

Ch1 уже доказала, что одинаковая внешняя фраза может скрывать разные неопределённости. Ch9 не повторяет этот тезис. Её новая функция — **live post-offer decision routing**.

## MECHANISM / CAUSAL CHAIN

`post-offer signal → seller jumps to one cause → generic objection response → cause remains unresolved / pressure increases / time wasted`.

Counter-mechanism:

`observable signal → 2–3 plausible competing hypotheses → one discriminating fact/question → route to the relevant prior tool / wait / accept no → stop when further action has no justified target`.

No claim that wording alone reveals buyer psychology.

## DECISION

Choose one of these action classes:

1. **VERIFY ONE FACT** — one neutral discriminating check can separate plausible causes;
2. **ROUTE TO PRIOR TOOL** — return only to the relevant earlier object:
   - service decision/boundary → Ch2;
   - evidence → Ch3;
   - process/participation → Ch4;
   - compatibility condition → Ch6;
   - economic construction → Ch8;
3. **WAIT FOR A REAL EVENT / DATE** — timing/priority is genuine and there is a concrete next condition;
4. **ENABLE A RELEVANT DECISION PARTICIPANT** — only where buyer confirms internal decision structure and participation is appropriate;
5. **ACCEPT NO / STOP** — buyer chose another option, does not want the service, or further follow-up has no justified basis.

No default action `handle objection`.

## ACTION / ARTIFACT

### Диагностика незавершённого решения
Internal ID: `Uncertainty / Objection Diagnostic`.

Status: **AUTHOR-CREATED SYNTHESIS**, not validated psychological taxonomy.

### Step 1 — СИГНАЛ
Record only observable event:
- exact phrase;
- silence/non-response after agreed moment;
- delay request;
- cheaper-term request;
- internal-review request;
- comparison request;
- explicit refusal.

No inferred cause in this field.

### Step 2 — КОНКУРИРУЮЩИЕ ГИПОТЕЗЫ
Generate only materially plausible alternatives. Candidate families:

1. **ЭКОНОМИКА / ФИНАНСОВЫЙ РИСК** — route to Ch8 only after verification;
2. **ОБЪЕКТ ПОКУПКИ / ГРАНИЦЫ** — result/scope/boundary issue;
3. **ОСНОВАНИЯ ВЫБРАТЬ ИСПОЛНИТЕЛЯ** — evidence/provider relevance gap;
4. **ПРОЦЕСС / УЧАСТИЕ** — future-delivery concern;
5. **УСЛОВИЯ СОВМЕСТИМОСТИ** — known fit/condition problem;
6. **ВРЕМЯ / ПРИОРИТЕТ / ТЕКУЩЕЕ СОСТОЯНИЕ** — rational postponement, switching cost, other priority or preference to keep current state;
7. **ВНУТРЕННЕЕ РЕШЕНИЕ** — relevant B2B stakeholder/alignment/approval condition;
8. **ДРУГАЯ АЛЬТЕРНАТИВА / НАСТОЯЩЕЕ НЕТ** — buyer simply prefers not to buy this offer/provider.

These are author synthesis, not scientific taxonomy.

### Step 3 — РАЗЛИЧАЮЩИЙ ФАКТ
For top 2–3 hypotheses ask:
> What one observable fact or neutral question would make one explanation more likely and another less likely?

One check may be enough. Do not conduct another full discovery interview.

### Step 4 — ДЕЙСТВИЕ
Choose action only after cause is sufficiently supported.

### Step 5 — STOP CONDITION
Stop pursuit when:
- buyer gives a clear no;
- no consent/basis for further follow-up;
- action cannot change the identified cause;
- deal is incompatible under Ch6;
- only pressure remains as a tactic.

## OBSERVABLE CHECK

Reader takes one live post-offer stall and can produce:

- exact observable signal;
- 2–3 plausible causes instead of one assumption;
- one discriminating fact/question;
- one action tied to evidence;
- one explicit stop condition.

Fail conditions:

- `дорого` automatically = price problem;
- `подумаю` automatically = lack of trust;
- seller asks five questions that cannot change action;
- every hypothesis routes to more persuasion;
- genuine no is treated as hidden objection;
- status quo/decision conflict is used as pop-psychology label without evidence;
- B2B seller invents hidden decision-makers not confirmed by buyer.

## EVIDENCE FUNCTION

### Anderson — Psychological Bulletin, 2003
`The Psychology of Doing Nothing: Forms of Decision Avoidance Result From Reason and Emotion`  
https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.129.1.139

Use:
- review integrates multiple forms of decision avoidance: choice deferral, status quo bias, omission bias, inaction inertia;
- supports multiple possible antecedents of inaction/deferral.

Boundary:
- not service-sales evidence;
- does not validate our eight hypotheses.

### Samuelson & Zeckhauser — Journal of Risk and Uncertainty, 1988
`Status Quo Bias in Decision Making`  
https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00055564

Use:
- supports current/default state as a real decision alternative in experiments and reported real-world plan selections.

Boundary:
- do not label every `не сейчас` as bias; staying put can be rational.

### Dhar — Journal of Consumer Research, 1997
`Consumer Preference for a No-Choice Option`  
https://doi.org/10.1086/209506

Use:
- seven studies support preference uncertainty as one possible contributor to choice deferral in studied contexts.

Boundary:
- consumer-choice context; no universal sales mapping.

### Evangelidis, Levav & Simonson — Management Science, 2023
`A Reexamination of the Impact of Decision Conflict on Choice Deferral`  
https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2022.4484

Use:
- 40 well-powered direct/conceptual replications, total n=26,703;
- casts doubt on robustness of earlier conflict→deferral findings.

Editorial consequence:
- chapter explicitly refuses a universal `conflict causes delay` law;
- scientific uncertainty strengthens competing-hypothesis design.

### Edelman–LinkedIn 2025 B2B Thought Leadership Impact Report
https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report

Use:
- nearly 2,000 management-level professionals;
- current commercial evidence that hidden/internal buyers and buying-group misalignment can stall B2B decisions;
- supports internal-decision hypothesis in B2B only.

Boundary:
- commercial survey, not causal peer-reviewed evidence;
- no universal transfer to B2C/Russia;
- recheck statistic/freshness before master if used numerically.

Detailed traceability: `../source-notes/09-ya-podumayu-sources.md`.

## RUSSIA APPLICATION

No cultural stereotype such as `российский клиент любит торговаться`.

### Individual/B2C
Often one person decides; do not invent stakeholder complexity. Real causes may include budget, timing, provider preference, family/partner input where buyer explicitly says so, or genuine no.

### Owner-led small B2B
Owner may combine user/payer/decision roles. Cash flow and priority can be real economic/timing causes; do not psychologize them.

### Multi-stakeholder B2B
Internal alignment/approval can be a real cause. Use only buyer-confirmed roles; do not circumvent the contact person or treat «hidden buyer» as licence to go around them.

Specific follow-up/privacy/electronic-marketing legal claims are NOT CORE and require fresh Russian check if later included.

## SCENE / CASE

Opening must NOT repeat Ch1 `three clients say я подумаю` structure.

Model/composite candidate:

A consultant sent an approved proposal after a detailed meeting. The buyer replies the next day: `Спасибо, предложение получили. Пока не готовы подтвердить, вернёмся позже.`

Seller is tempted to send a discount/extra case. Instead, the chapter reconstructs what is actually known: before the proposal the task and fit were clear; after it, the only new fact is a delayed decision. Competing hypotheses are generated, then one discriminating check changes the next action.

Function: **post-offer causal routing**, not Ch1 taxonomy.
