## 2. Chapter 1 — ADMITTED TO WRITING

### Evidence foundation

Сильная опора уже есть в `RESEARCH-MAP.md`:

- professional services трудно специфицировать/сравнивать/оценивать ex ante;
- perceived risk связан с uncertainty/intangibility;
- credence qualities усиливают information asymmetry;
- клиенты используют observable ex-ante signals для screening.

Key source:

Pemer & Skjølsvik, `The cues that matter: Screening for quality signals in the ex ante phase of buying professional services`, Journal of Business Research (2019).  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0148296319300955

Important boundary: конкретная `Buyer Uncertainty Map` — author synthesis. Не выдавать её за universal scientific taxonomy.

**Decision:** `WRITING_ALLOWED=YES`.

## 3. Chapter 5 — diagnosis before proposal

### New/confirmed evidence

Research on salesperson value-opportunity recognition in solution selling supports the importance of deep customer/business knowledge in early solution-selling phases:

`Drivers and outcomes of salespersons’ value opportunity recognition competence in solution selling`, Journal of Personal Selling & Sales Management (2020).  
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/08853134.2020.1778484

The study uses multi-level data from 799 salespeople / 29 sales organizations and finds customer knowledge more important than technical knowledge for value-opportunity recognition.

Additional background exists on questioning skill as an information-seeking/needs-diagnosis capability, but avoid relying on generic discovery-question lists or copying SPIN.

### Boundary

Evidence supports **understanding customer context before solution design**, not a universal discovery-call script.

**Status:** blocker reduced, contract still needs final synthesis before `YES`.

## 4. Chapter 6 — fit / qualification

### Stronger independent foundation

Buell, Campbell & Frei, `The Customer May Not Always Be Right: Customer Compatibility and Service Performance`, Management Science 67(3).  
https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3596

Defines customer compatibility as fit between customer needs and capabilities of the operation serving them; reports relationships with customer experience and firm performance.

Additional professional-services evidence:

`Successful cooperation in professional services: What characteristics should the customer have?`, Industrial Marketing Management (1995).  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/001985019400042U

Client participation/integration evidence already recorded in Architecture research addendum.

### Boundary

These sources support fit/participation as legitimate variables. They do **not** validate a universal BANT-like scorecard.

Qualification / No-Go Criteria remains transparent author synthesis.

**Decision:** independent Ch6 function remains credible; hard MERGE test with Ch5 stays active.

## 5. Chapter 7 — proposal as decision document

### Evidence

`The ambiguous proposal evaluation problem`, Decision Support Systems 136 (2020).  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167923620301147

Shows ambiguity in proposals can complicate comparison/ranking in complex project selection. This is procurement/decision-support evidence, not a direct sales-proposal playbook.

Ex-ante professional-service signal research (Pemer & Skjølsvik) plus process-visibility evidence can support what information buyers use before selection.

### Boundary

Do not claim one universal proposal template raises conversion. The `Offer Decision Sheet` should be framed as an author-created completeness/clarity diagnostic derived from the book mechanism.

**Status:** research path now viable; final contract synthesis still required.

## 6. Chapter 8 — price, scope and risk

### Strong evidence

`Price negotiating for services: elucidating the ambivalent effects on customers’ negotiation aspirations`, Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science.  
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11747-019-00676-4

The paper explicitly links service heterogeneity/quality uncertainty to perceived product risk and describes price negotiation as one potential risk-reduction response.

Older service price-quality evidence also shows price may function as a quality cue only under some conditions, not as a universal rule:

`The Effects of Awareness and Quality Risk on the Price-Quality Relationship Among Services`.  
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1300/J127v01n01_06

### Boundary

Absolute claims `дороже = качественнее` / `цена должна отражать ценность` are forbidden. Ch8 must treat price together with scope, obligations, uncertainty and distribution of risk.

Russia-specific payment/staging/consumer/legal details remain fresh-check blockers.

**Status:** core mechanism strengthened; local/guarantee boundaries still open.

## 7. Chapter 9 — hesitation / no-decision

### Evidence

HBR summary of large-scale recorded sales-conversation research:

Dixon & McKenna, `Stop Losing Sales to Customer Indecision` (2022).  
https://hbr.org/2022/06/stop-losing-sales-to-customer-indecision

Use as professional/commercial research, not neutral universal law.

Current B2B evidence on buying uncertainty/consensus:

Gartner, `Help B2B Buyers Navigate Change Management for Complex Purchases` (2024).  
https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/5088931

Gartner 2025 survey on unhealthy conflict in B2B buying groups:  
https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-05-07-gartner-sales-survey-finds-74-percent-of-b2b-buyer-teams-demonstrate-unhealthy-conflict-during-the-decision-process

General status-quo/decision-avoidance literature can explain inertia, but product/channel-specific studies must not be generalized mechanically to every service purchase.

### Boundary

The exact `Uncertainty/Objection Diagnostic` taxonomy remains author synthesis. Ch9 must not become «7 типов возражений» presented as science.

**Status:** blocker reduced; final taxonomy/action branches still need refinement.
