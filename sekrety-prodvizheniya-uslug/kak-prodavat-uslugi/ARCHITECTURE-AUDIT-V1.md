## 2. Definition fit

Утверждённый Definition требует:

- buyer uncertainty / perceived risk как central spine;
- top-tier world-class benchmark;
- original contribution;
- strong practical system;
- Russian application;
- zero-overlap;
- protected future books.

Architecture v1 сохраняет spine во всех 10 chapters. Нет отдельной главы, которая превращает книгу в generic lead generation, personal brand, advertising, SMM/SEO, CRM, customer service или online-course funnel.

**Result: PASS.**

## 3. Book-level causal progression

Предлагаемая последовательность:

1. диагностировать uncertainty;
2. определить decision object услуги;
3. подобрать evidence;
4. сделать future process observable;
5. диагностировать задачу до offer;
6. проверить fit/qualification;
7. собрать proposal as decision object;
8. design price/scope/risk;
9. диагностировать hesitation;
10. учиться на wins/losses.

Каждый шаг меняет отдельный объект решения продавца.

**Result: PASS-DRAFT.**

## 4. Zero-overlap audit

### Ch1 vs Ch9

Risk: обе chapters используют uncertainty taxonomy.

Separation:
- Ch1 — book-wide diagnosis до вмешательства: «что именно трудно купить?»;
- Ch9 — post-offer hypothesis testing: «что именно осталось unresolved за surface objection?».

Allowed only if Ch9 не повторяет introductory taxonomy lesson и immediately works with decision branches.

**Result: PASS WITH WRITING CONSTRAINT.**

### Ch3 vs Ch4

- Ch3: evidence about provider/relevance/quality;
- Ch4: visibility of future delivery/roles/checkpoints.

**Result: PASS.**

### Ch5 vs Ch6

Highest overlap risk.

- Ch5: `what is the actual task and what information is missing?`;
- Ch6: `can this provider/client/scope/participation configuration produce a good service outcome?`.

New evidence on customer compatibility and professional-service client participation supports independent fit as a legitimate service variable. The qualification tool remains author synthesis.

**Decision:** KEEP Ch6 in Architecture v1, but retain hard MERGE test. If chapter draft cannot remain independent without repeating Ch5, Architecture is amended to 9 chapters.

**Result: CONDITIONAL PASS.**

### Ch7 vs Ch8

- Ch7 integrates decision information into proposal;
- Ch8 owns economic terms, scope and risk allocation.

Price may appear as a field in Ch7 but reasoning belongs only to Ch8.

**Result: PASS.**

### Ch8 vs Ch9

- Ch8: economic transaction design;
- Ch9: diagnostic response to any unresolved hesitation.

Ch9 may reference price uncertainty but must not reteach negotiation/risk design.

**Result: PASS WITH BOUNDARY.**

### Ch9 vs Ch10

- Ch9 before outcome;
- Ch10 after outcome, fact/inference/pattern learning.

**Result: PASS.**

## 5. Practical-value audit

Each chapter has an observable business artifact:

1. Buyer Uncertainty Map
2. Service Decision Definition
3. Evidence Inventory + Gap Map
4. Process Visibility Map
5. Diagnostic Conversation Map
6. Qualification / No-Go Criteria
7. Offer Decision Sheet + revised proposal
8. Price–Scope–Risk Map
9. Uncertainty/Objection Diagnostic
10. Lost-Deal Learning Loop

`Personal Service Sales System` is explicitly integration, not an eleventh duplicate framework.

The outputs form a sequence from diagnosis to system learning and pass the next-business-day test at Architecture level.

Risk: too many worksheets could make the book feel like a course/manual rather than top-tier nonfiction. Writing must embed artifacts in argument and use only the minimum necessary fields.

**Result: PASS WITH PROSE/UX CONSTRAINT.**

## 6. World-class benchmark audit

### Against `Selling the Invisible`

Not a rewrite: Architecture uses causal diagnostics, business artifacts and buyer-decision system rather than independent service-marketing observations.

### Against `The Trusted Advisor`

Trust is not central slogan/framework; it is decomposed into evidence/process/fit/reliability inputs.

### Against `SPIN Selling`

Questioning is only Ch5 and no SPIN sequence is copied; system covers service definition, evidence, process, offer, economics and learning.

### Against `Book Yourself Solid`

Lead generation/personal brand/client attraction are protected out of book; implementation focus is narrower and deeper on decision mechanics.

### Against `The Challenger Sale`

Architecture seeks a memorable thesis and evidence discipline without adopting Challenger archetype or Teach–Tailor–Take Control.

**Result: PASS-DRAFT / ORIGINAL CONTRIBUTION VISIBLE.**
