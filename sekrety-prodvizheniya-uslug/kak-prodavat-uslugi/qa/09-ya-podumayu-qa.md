## 4. Research integrity / scientific humility

**STRONG PASS.**

Traceability: `../source-notes/09-ya-podumayu-sources.md`.

Evidence functions:

- Anderson (Psychological Bulletin 2003) — decision avoidance includes multiple forms/antecedents;
- Samuelson & Zeckhauser (1988) — status quo can be a genuine decision alternative;
- Dhar (JCR 1997) — preference uncertainty can contribute to choice deferral in studied contexts;
- Evangelidis, Levav & Simonson (Management Science 2023) — 40 well-powered replications, total n=26,703, cast doubt on robust classic conflict→deferral effect;
- Edelman–LinkedIn 2025 — current commercial B2B evidence for hidden/internal buyers and buying-group misalignment as a possible cause of deal stall.

Strong editorial choice:
- manuscript includes the replication correction instead of presenting a famous behavioral claim as settled law;
- exact eight hypothesis families are explicitly presented as author synthesis, not scientific taxonomy.

Not claimed:
- wording reveals psychology;
- all delay is bias;
- conflict reliably causes deferral;
- 40% B2B statistic applies to Russia/B2C universally;
- a particular objection phrase maps to one cause.
