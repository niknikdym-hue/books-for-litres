## 5. Practical value

**PASS.**

Artifact: **Offer Decision Sheet / Карта решения по предложению**.

Eight audit fields:
1. situation/task;
2. what is being bought;
3. relevant provider evidence;
4. decision-relevant future process;
5. critical compatibility conditions;
6. investment/basic payment terms;
7. material non-guaranteed/external-condition boundary;
8. next decision/action.

Key practical feature: the tool audits an **existing real proposal** rather than requiring reader to create a new template from zero.

Observable buyer-retell test: a relevant reader who did not attend the meeting should be able to reconstruct the basic purchase decision without seller explanation.
