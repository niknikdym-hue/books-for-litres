## 3. Research integrity

**PASS WITH TRANSFER LIMITS.**

Evidence functions:

### Buell, Campbell & Frei — Management Science
- customer compatibility defined as fit between customer needs and capabilities of the operations serving them;
- reported empirical relationships between compatibility, satisfaction and firm performance in studied banking/QSR contexts.

Use boundary:
- supports compatibility as a real service-operation variable;
- no numeric transfer to professional services;
- no validation of our six-field qualification artifact.

### Fu et al. — PLOS ONE, 2023
- 58 project managers + 171 consultants in professional-service teams;
- client involvement related to team performance and idea creativity in the study;
- team bonding capital moderated effects;
- paper explicitly treats client involvement as condition-dependent and potentially complicating.

Use boundary:
- supports participation as a material delivery condition;
- no claim that more participation is universally better;
- no predictive project-success score.
